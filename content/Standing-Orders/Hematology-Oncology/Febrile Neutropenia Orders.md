# Febrile Neutropenia — ER Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local antibiogram and the attending. **Cultures then empiric antipseudomonal β-lactam WITHIN 1 HOUR — do not wait for a source.** Signs are blunted. **No rectal exams/suppositories/temps.** Septic shock → [[Sepsis (Sepsis-3)]] bundle.

## 🔴 One Day
- [ ] Confirm: **temp ≥ 38.3 °C (or ≥ 38.0 × 1 h) + ANC < 500** (or < 1000 falling)
- [ ] **Blood cultures (peripheral + each central-line lumen) BEFORE antibiotics**; CBC + diff, U&E, LFT, CRP/procalcitonin, lactate, urinalysis + culture, **CXR**; culture any focal site
- [ ] **Empiric antibiotics within 1 h: antipseudomonal β-lactam monotherapy — Cefepime 2 g IV q8h OR Piperacillin-tazobactam 4.5 g IV q6h OR Meropenem 1 g IV q8h** (carbapenem if ESBL risk)
- [ ] **Add Vancomycin** ONLY for: haemodynamic instability, catheter/skin-soft-tissue infection, pneumonia, MRSA colonisation, severe mucositis
- [ ] Focused exam for source (mouth, perianal — inspect only, skin/lines, chest, abdomen); **NO DRE/suppositories**
- [ ] **MASCC score** → **≥ 21 low risk** (consider oral **fluoroquinolone + amoxicillin-clavulanate**/outpatient if reliable) · **≤ 20 high risk** (admit IV)
- [ ] Resuscitate sepsis/shock if present; IV fluids; O₂

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Admit (protective precautions; ICU if unstable) — Dx: febrile neutropenia (ANC ___, MASCC ___)
- [ ] Vitals + daily ANC; reassess at 48–72 h — **de-escalate vancomycin if no Gram-positive isolate**; tailor to cultures
- [ ] **Persistent/recurrent fever after ~4–7 days** in prolonged neutropenia → **empiric antifungal** (echinocandin/mould-active azole) + fungal markers (galactomannan/β-D-glucan) ± CT chest/sinuses
- [ ] Source control: remove/treat infected line as indicated; treat mucositis
- [ ] **G-CSF** in selected high-risk/deteriorating patients (not routine)
- [ ] Continue antibiotics until afebrile + neutrophil recovery (or defined course for identified infection)

> [!info]- Why
> Neutropenic patients can't localise or contain infection, so empiric broad Gram-negative (incl. Pseudomonas) cover within an hour is the intervention that saves lives; vancomycin and antifungals add toxicity/resistance and are reserved for specific triggers. MASCC identifies the minority safe for oral/outpatient care. Rectal instrumentation risks translocation. Verified vs IDSA/ASCO FN guidance + Thai Soc Hematology 2567.
