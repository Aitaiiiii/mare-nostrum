# Electrical Injury — ER Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local protocol and the attending. Ensure power source OFF before contact. **Visible burns underestimate deep injury** — resuscitate for internal damage. **Continuous cardiac monitoring; treat as trauma. Lightning → prolonged CPR + reverse triage.**

## 🔴 One Day
- [ ] Scene safety (power off); ABCs; **cardiac arrest → ACLS** ([[CPR - ACLS]]); treat as **trauma** (c-spine/ATLS)
- [ ] **ECG + continuous cardiac monitoring**; troponin if cardiac concern
- [ ] Labs: **CK + urine myoglobin**, U&E/creatinine, **K⁺**, venous gas, CBC, coagulation
- [ ] **Rhabdomyolysis: aggressive IV fluids titrated to urine output ~1–1.5 mL/kg/h** (higher if pigmenturia) — do NOT under-resuscitate (deep injury ≫ skin burn)
- [ ] Assess limbs for **compartment syndrome → fasciotomy**; wound care; **tetanus prophylaxis**; analgesia
- [ ] Trauma survey/imaging (c-spine, fractures from tetany/fall)
- [ ] **Admit if:** ECG abnormality, arrest/LOC, chest pain/arrhythmia, **high-voltage** exposure, transthoracic current path, significant burns/rhabdomyolysis

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Admit (monitored/ICU) or, for **low-voltage + normal ECG + asymptomatic + no LOC**, discharge after observation
- [ ] Continue fluids + monitor **CK/K⁺/renal function** (myoglobinuric AKI); serial limb/compartment checks
- [ ] Serial surgical assessment of deep tissue (debridement/amputation as needed)
- [ ] Ophthalmology (cataracts) + neurology follow-up for delayed sequelae

> [!info]- Why
> Current injures deep tissue (muscle/vessels/nerves) far beyond the skin, so fluid needs and rhabdomyolysis risk exceed burn-formula estimates; the heart is at risk of arrhythmia (monitoring), and tetany/falls cause occult trauma. Lightning arrests are often recoverable respiratory arrests (reverse triage, prolonged CPR). Verified vs electrical-injury guidance.
