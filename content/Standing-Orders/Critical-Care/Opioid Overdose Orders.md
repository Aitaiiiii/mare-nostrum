# Opioid Overdose — ER Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local protocol and the attending. **Oxygenate/ventilate FIRST.** Titrate naloxone to breathing, not arousal. **Long-acting opioids (methadone/MR) re-sedate → infusion + prolonged observation.** Check glucose; screen co-ingestants.

## 🔴 One Day
- [ ] ABC; **support ventilation (BVM/O₂)**; capillary glucose
- [ ] **Naloxone 0.04–0.4 mg IV, repeat/double every 2–3 min** to adequate spontaneous respiration (low start in chronic users; IM/IN if no IV; up to ~2 mg peri-arrest)
- [ ] **Naloxone infusion at ~⅔ of the effective cumulative bolus dose/hour** if repeated boluses needed (long-acting/recurrent)
- [ ] ECG (**QTc — methadone**; QRS if co-ingestant); venous gas
- [ ] Screen deliberate overdose: **paracetamol + salicylate levels**, coingestants; CK if "found down"
- [ ] Manage complications: non-cardiogenic pulmonary oedema (supportive), aspiration; **seizures (tramadol/meperidine) → benzodiazepine**; **torsades (methadone) → magnesium**

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Observe monitored; **admit if a second naloxone dose was needed or fails ~4–6 h observation**; ICU for long-acting/MR ingestion or infusion
- [ ] Continuous SpO₂/RR/sedation monitoring; titrate/wean naloxone infusion to respiratory drive
- [ ] Watch for re-sedation and withdrawal; supportive care
- [ ] **Psychiatric/risk assessment** (intentional); harm-reduction + **take-home naloxone** counselling; addiction referral

> [!info]- Why
> Death is from hypoventilation, so airway/oxygenation is primary and naloxone is titrated to restore breathing while avoiding precipitated withdrawal; because naloxone is shorter-acting than many opioids, long-acting ingestions re-sedate and need an infusion plus extended observation. Verified vs opioid-toxicity guidance.
