# Heat Stroke — ER Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local protocol and the attending. **Cooling IS the treatment — cool to core < 39 °C within 30 min** ("cool first, transport second"). **Antipyretics and dantrolene do NOT work.** Measure a **core (rectal) temperature** and stop cooling at ~39 °C (avoid overshoot). Anticipate rhabdomyolysis/AKI, DIC, hepatic failure.

## 🔴 One Day
- [ ] **Continuous core (rectal) temperature**; ABCs; high-flow O₂; airway protection if coma/seizing
- [ ] **Rapid cooling immediately — cold/ice-water immersion (fastest)**; if unavailable → evaporative (spray + fans), ice packs to neck/axillae/groin, cooling blankets, cold IV fluids; **target core < 39 °C within 30 min, then stop**
- [ ] Benzodiazepine for shivering/seizures/agitation; **isotonic crystalloid** for hypotension (titrated)
- [ ] Labs: CBC, coagulation/DIC screen, electrolytes (**K⁺**), BUN/Cr, glucose, **CK** (rhabdo), LFT, ABG/lactate; ECG; capillary glucose
- [ ] Exclude mimics: sepsis, thyroid storm, toxidromes (NMS/serotonin/anticholinergic), CNS infection
- [ ] **Do NOT give antipyretics or dantrolene**

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Admit ICU — Dx: heat stroke (exertional/classic)
- [ ] Monitor core temp (avoid rebound hyper/hypothermia); continuous cardiac + neuro monitoring
- [ ] **Manage rhabdomyolysis → AKI** (fluids, CK/K⁺/UOP — see [[Rhabdomyolysis]]), **DIC**, hepatic injury, ARDS; organ support
- [ ] Treat the cause/context (exertional vs classic); prevention counselling

> [!info]- Why
> Mortality tracks time above ~40 °C, so rapid cooling to < 39 °C within 30 minutes (immersion is fastest) is the intervention; antipyretics fail because the set-point is normal, and dantrolene (unlike malignant hyperthermia) does not help. Rhabdomyolysis, DIC, and hepatic/renal failure are the downstream killers to anticipate. Verified vs 2024 exertional-heat-stroke evidence.
