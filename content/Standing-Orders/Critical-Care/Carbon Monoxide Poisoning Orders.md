# Carbon Monoxide Poisoning — ER Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local protocol / hyperbaric service. **Pulse oximetry is falsely normal — measure COHb.** 100% oxygen immediately (before confirmation). Suspect in multiple co-located people ill together. Smoke inhalation + high lactate → concurrent cyanide.

## 🔴 One Day
- [ ] **100% oxygen via non-rebreather mask immediately** (ETT if intubated); remove from source
- [ ] **Carboxyhaemoglobin (COHb) by co-oximetry** (venous/arterial); ABG/**lactate**, ECG + troponin, CBC, electrolytes, glucose, pregnancy test
- [ ] Continue 100% O₂ **until COHb < ~3% AND ≥ 6 hours** (extended in pregnancy)
- [ ] **Hyperbaric oxygen referral** if: **LOC, neurological signs/deficit, cardiac ischaemia/arrhythmia, metabolic acidosis, COHb > 25% (urgent > 40%), or pregnancy (lower threshold)** — arrange transfer early, don't delay 100% O₂
- [ ] **[Smoke inhalation + high lactate/soot] treat concurrent cyanide → hydroxocobalamin** (avoid nitrites)
- [ ] Treat seizures, myocardial ischaemia, rhabdomyolysis; correct acidosis (improves with O₂)

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Admit (monitored/ICU if severe) — Dx: CO poisoning (COHb ___%)
- [ ] Continue oxygen per COHb/duration; serial COHb, ECG/troponin, lactate
- [ ] **Counsel + follow for delayed neurological sequelae** (days–weeks: cognitive/psychiatric/movement)
- [ ] **Source remediation before discharge home** (CO detector, appliance/heater check) — prevent household recurrence/deaths
- [ ] Psychiatric assessment if intentional

> [!info]- Why
> CO causes tissue hypoxia with a normal SpO₂/PaO₂, so diagnosis needs co-oximetry and treatment is oxygen (which halves the CO half-life); HBO is reserved for high-risk features to reduce delayed neurocognitive injury. Enclosed-space fires add cyanide. Fixing the source prevents further household poisonings. Verified vs CO-poisoning guidance.
