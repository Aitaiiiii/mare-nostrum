# Toxic Alcohol Poisoning — ER Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local protocol / poison centre (1367). **Treat on suspicion — don't wait for levels.** High anion gap + high osmolar gap + inebriation without ethanol smell = toxic alcohol. Methanol → blindness; ethylene glycol → AKI. **Cluster of acidosis + visual loss = methanol outbreak → notify public health.**

## 🔴 One Day
- [ ] Labs: **anion gap + osmolar gap** (measured − calculated osmolality), ABG/VBG, electrolytes, **calcium** (EG), renal function, glucose, lactate, **ethanol level**, **paracetamol + salicylate**; send methanol/ethylene-glycol levels if available
- [ ] Urinalysis (calcium oxalate crystals — EG)
- [ ] **Block ADH — Fomepizole 15 mg/kg IV loading, then 10 mg/kg IV q12h** (×4, then 15 mg/kg q12h) — continue until level < 20 mg/dL + acidosis resolved
  - If fomepizole unavailable → **ethanol infusion** (target ~100–150 mg/dL), monitor levels/glucose
- [ ] **Sodium bicarbonate** for severe acidaemia
- [ ] **Cofactors: folinic/folic acid (methanol); thiamine + pyridoxine (ethylene glycol)**
- [ ] **Nephrology — haemodialysis** for severe acidosis, end-organ injury (visual/methanol; AKI/EG), very high levels, or deterioration (continue fomepizole, adjust around HD)
- [ ] **[EG] treat hypocalcaemia only if symptomatic** (calcium may worsen oxalate deposition); ophthalmology for methanol

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Admit ICU — Dx: toxic alcohol poisoning (?methanol/?ethylene glycol)
- [ ] Continue ADH blockade + cofactors; serial gases/anion gap, renal function, calcium
- [ ] Monitor for visual loss (methanol), AKI (EG); dialysis until acidosis cleared + levels low
- [ ] **Notify poison centre + public health if methanol outbreak** (illicit liquor); psychiatric assessment if deliberate

> [!info]- Why
> The parent alcohols are relatively harmless; toxicity is from ADH-generated metabolites (formic acid → blindness; oxalate → AKI), so early ADH blockade (fomepizole/ethanol) prevents injury and dialysis removes toxin + corrects acidosis. Cofactors divert metabolism to non-toxic products. Verified vs toxic-alcohol guidance.
