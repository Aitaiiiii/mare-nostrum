# Accidental Hypothermia — ER Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local protocol and the attending. **"Not dead until warm and dead" — continue prolonged CPR; rewarm before declaring death.** **Handle gently** (VF risk). Hypothermic arrest → ECLS/ECMO rewarming. Limit defib/ACLS drugs until core > ~30 °C.

## 🔴 One Day
- [ ] **Low-reading core (rectal/oesophageal) temperature**; continuous cardiac monitoring, SpO₂; **minimise rough handling**
- [ ] ECG (**Osborn J waves**, bradyarrhythmia); labs: glucose, **electrolytes (K⁺)**, coagulation, ABG, CK, thyroid/toxicology per cause
- [ ] Remove wet clothing; insulate; **warmed IV fluids**; treat hypoglycaemia
- [ ] **Rewarm by severity:** mild → passive external (+ forced warm air); moderate → active external + warm humidified O₂/warm fluids; **severe/unstable → active internal ± extracorporeal**; target ~1–2 °C/h; watch **afterdrop**
- [ ] **[Cardiac arrest] prolonged CPR + transfer for ECLS/ECMO rewarming**; withhold/limit defibrillation + ACLS drugs until core > ~30 °C, then standard [[CPR - ACLS]]
- [ ] **Do not declare death until rewarmed** (unless lethal injury / markedly high K⁺ = prognostic futility)

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Admit (ICU if moderate/severe/arrest) — Dx: accidental hypothermia (stage ___, cause ___)
- [ ] Continue controlled rewarming; monitor for afterdrop, rewarming acidosis/hyperkalaemia, arrhythmia, coagulopathy
- [ ] Treat the cause/associated conditions (hypoglycaemia, hypothyroidism/[[Myxedema Coma]], sepsis, drowning, intoxication, trauma)
- [ ] Monitor for rhabdomyolysis/AKI/pancreatitis

> [!info]- Why
> Deep hypothermia is neuroprotective, so resuscitation is prolonged and death is declared only after rewarming; the cold heart is irritable (VF on rough handling) and unresponsive to drugs/defibrillation until warmer, and ECLS is the fastest, best-outcome rewarming for arrest. Afterdrop and rewarming hyperkalaemia/acidosis follow. Verified vs hypothermia resuscitation guidance.
