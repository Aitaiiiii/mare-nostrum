# Beta-Blocker / CCB Overdose — ER Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local protocol / poison centre (1367). **Sustained-release preparations delay toxicity — monitor long.** Start HIET early (with a vasopressor, not alone). HIET → hypoglycaemia + hypokalaemia (monitor). Refractory shock → lipid emulsion/ECMO.

## 🔴 One Day
- [ ] Continuous cardiac monitor + ECG (bradycardia, blocks, QRS/QT); large-bore IV; **glucose** (hyperglycaemia → CCB)
- [ ] **IV crystalloid** for hypotension; **atropine** for symptomatic bradycardia
- [ ] Activated charcoal if early/airway protected; **whole-bowel irrigation for sustained-release** ingestion
- [ ] **Calcium** (gluconate/chloride, repeat/infusion) — esp. CCB
- [ ] **Glucagon** bolus → infusion — esp. BB (antiemetic/airway — causes vomiting)
- [ ] **HIET: insulin 1 U/kg IV bolus, then 1–10 U/kg/h infusion** + **dextrose to euglycaemia** + **K⁺ repletion**; **glucose q20min → hourly**, monitor K⁺
- [ ] **Vasopressors (noradrenaline/adrenaline)** for shock (combine with HIET)
- [ ] Refractory: **IV lipid emulsion** (lipophilic BB), methylene blue (vasoplegia), pacing, **VA-ECMO** referral; propranolol wide-QRS → **bicarbonate**; sotalol torsades → **magnesium**
- [ ] Labs: electrolytes, venous gas/lactate, renal function, **paracetamol + salicylate**

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Admit monitored/ICU — Dx: BB/CCB overdose (agent ___, ?sustained-release)
- [ ] Continue HIET + vasopressors + calcium/glucagon titrated to perfusion; **prolonged monitoring for SR preparations**
- [ ] Serial glucose + K⁺ (HIET effects); ECG; lactate/perfusion markers
- [ ] Wean therapies as toxicity resolves; psychiatric/risk assessment (deliberate)

> [!info]- Why
> Toxicity causes bradycardia + pump failure; calcium and glucagon give partial support, but **HIET** restores myocardial energy substrate and contractility and is the workhorse in severe cases (combined with a vasopressor). SR preparations crash late. Lipid emulsion/ECMO bridge refractory shock until the drug clears. Verified vs BB/CCB toxicity guidance.
