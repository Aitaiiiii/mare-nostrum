# Acute Decompensated Heart Failure — Admission Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local protocol and the attending. Assess the **Stevenson profile (warm/cold × wet/dry)** — most are **warm-wet (→ IV diuretic)**; **cold-wet/low-output → inotrope + specialist**. Find the precipitant (FAILURE). Hypertensive flash pulmonary oedema → [[SCAPE (Flash Pulmonary Edema)]] (NIV + nitrate). Watch K⁺/renal with diuresis.

## 🔴 One Day
- [ ] **NT-proBNP/BNP**; ECG (AF, ischaemia, LBBB → CRT); CXR (congestion, effusion); **CBC, electrolytes (K⁺/Mg²⁺/Na⁺), BUN/Cr**, troponin, TFT, glucose; consider POCUS (B-lines, IVC, LV)
- [ ] O₂ if SpO₂ < 90%; **NIV/CPAP** for respiratory distress/pulmonary oedema
- [ ] **IV loop diuretic — furosemide**: **1–2.5× the total daily home oral dose IV** (naïve: 20–40 mg IV), bolus q12h or infusion; titrate to UOP > 0.5 mL/kg/h + decongestion (DOSE trial)
- [ ] **[Hypertensive/SCAPE] IV nitroglycerin** (afterload reduction) + NIV — see [[SCAPE (Flash Pulmonary Edema)]]
- [ ] **[Cold/low-output/cardiogenic shock] inotrope/vasopressor** (dobutamine/milrinone ± noradrenaline) + cardiology; consider [[MCS (Mechanical Circulatory Support)]] — see [[Inotrope & Vasopressor Infusions]]
- [ ] Strict I/O + daily weight; urinary catheter if needed; **fluid restrict (~1.5 L/day) + sodium restrict**; treat the precipitant (ACS, arrhythmia, infection, non-adherence, NSAIDs)

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Admit (monitored if unstable) — Dx: ADHF (HFrEF/HFmrEF/HFpEF, Stevenson profile ___, precipitant ___)
- [ ] Daily weight, strict I/O, vitals; **daily electrolytes + renal function** (watch hypokalaemia/AKI); titrate diuretic to euvolaemia then convert to oral
- [ ] **HFrEF (EF ≤ 40%) — start/continue the 4 pillars as tolerated (BP/K⁺/renal permitting):**
  - **ARNI (sacubitril-valsartan)** or ACE-I/ARB
  - **Beta-blocker** (carvedilol/bisoprolol/metoprolol succinate) — start/continue when euvolaemic, not in cardiogenic shock
  - **MRA** (spironolactone/eplerenone) — if K⁺ < 5.0 and eGFR adequate
  - **SGLT2 inhibitor** (dapagliflozin/empagliflozin) — regardless of diabetes
- [ ] HFpEF/HFmrEF: diuretic for congestion + **SGLT2 inhibitor**; manage comorbidities (AF, HTN)
- [ ] Anticoagulation if AF/thrombus; device assessment (ICD/CRT) per EF/QRS after optimisation; cardiac rehab; vaccination; HF education (daily weight, salt/fluid, sick-day rules)

> [!info]- Why
> Decongestion with adequate IV loop diuretic relieves the warm-wet majority; the Stevenson profile flags the cold/low-output patient who needs inotropes not more diuretic. The four guideline pillars (ARNI/BB/MRA/SGLT2i) reduce HFrEF mortality and are started/continued during admission. SCAPE is an afterload problem (nitrate + NIV). Verified vs ESC 2021/2023 + AHA/ACC/HFSA 2022 + HFCT.
