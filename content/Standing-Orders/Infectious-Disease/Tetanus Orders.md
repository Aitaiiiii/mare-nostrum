# Tetanus — ICU/Admission Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local protocol and the attending. **Autonomic instability + laryngospasm are the killers → ICU, airway protection.** Metronidazole (NOT penicillin — GABA antagonist). Minimise stimulation (dark, quiet room). Vaccinate — the disease is non-immunising.

## 🔴 One Day
- [ ] Clinical diagnosis (trismus, risus sardonicus, spasms; spatula test) — treat now, don't wait for tests
- [ ] **Human tetanus immunoglobulin (TIG) single dose ~3000–6000 U IM** ASAP (neutralises unbound toxin)
- [ ] **Wound debridement** (remove devitalised/contaminated tissue)
- [ ] **Metronidazole 500 mg IV q6–8h** (preferred over penicillin)
- [ ] **Benzodiazepine (diazepam/midazolam) titrated/infusion** to control spasms; **magnesium sulfate infusion** for spasms + autonomic instability
- [ ] **Airway assessment — low threshold to intubate** (laryngospasm/severe spasms); ventilate ± neuromuscular blockade + sedation if refractory
- [ ] **Quiet, dark, low-stimulation environment**; continuous cardiac monitoring
- [ ] Labs: CK (rhabdomyolysis), electrolytes (incl. Mg/Ca), renal function; exclude mimics
- [ ] **Start tetanus toxoid vaccination series** (+ wound-based prophylaxis)

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Admit **ICU** — Dx: generalised tetanus
- [ ] Continue benzodiazepine ± magnesium; titrate to spasm control; prolonged course expected
- [ ] **Manage autonomic storm** (magnesium; short-acting labetalol/esmolol; avoid abrupt swings)
- [ ] Ventilatory/airway support; nutrition (NG/enteral); VTE prophylaxis; pressure-area + bowel/bladder care
- [ ] Monitor for rhabdomyolysis, fractures, secondary infection
- [ ] Complete 3-dose toxoid schedule during/after recovery; patient + community education

> [!info]- Why
> Tetanospasmin irreversibly disinhibits motor and autonomic neurons, so antitoxin only limits progression while spasms and autonomic instability must be controlled through a weeks-long ICU course; penicillin can worsen spasms (GABA antagonism), so metronidazole is preferred. Stimulation triggers spasms. Natural infection gives no immunity, so vaccination is essential. Verified vs standard tetanus management.
