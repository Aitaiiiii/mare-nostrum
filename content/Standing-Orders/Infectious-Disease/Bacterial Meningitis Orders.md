# Acute Bacterial Meningitis — ER/Admission Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local antibiogram and the attending. **Time-critical: cultures → dexamethasone → antibiotics within 1 h. Never delay antibiotics for CT/LP.** Dexamethasone must be given **with or just before** the first antibiotic. Purpuric rash/shock = meningococcal sepsis → droplet isolation + contact prophylaxis.

## 🔴 One Day
- [ ] **Blood cultures ×2 STAT** (before antibiotics)
- [ ] **Dexamethasone 10 mg IV STAT** (with or 10–20 min before first antibiotic), then q6h × 4 days — continue only if pneumococcal
- [ ] **Empiric antibiotics STAT (within 1 h):**
  - **Ceftriaxone 2 g IV q12h + Vancomycin** (load + level-guided)
  - **+ Ampicillin 2 g IV q4h** if **> 50 / immunocompromised / pregnant / alcoholic** (Listeria)
  - Post-neurosurgery/shunt/trauma: **Vancomycin + Cefepime 2 g IV q8h (or Meropenem 2 g IV q8h)**
  - **+ Aciclovir 10 mg/kg IV q8h** if HSV encephalitis possible
- [ ] **CT head before LP ONLY if:** immunocompromised, CNS disease history, **new seizure (≤ 1 wk)**, papilloedema, ↓consciousness, or focal deficit — otherwise **LP first** (antibiotics/steroid already given)
- [ ] **LP: opening pressure + CSF** for cell count/diff, glucose (+ paired serum glucose), protein, Gram stain, culture; **PCR (meningo/pneumo/HSV), CrAg, India ink, AFB/Xpert MTB, VDRL** per suspicion
- [ ] Labs: CBC, electrolytes, glucose, BUN/Cr, LFT, coagulation, procalcitonin/CRP, **HIV test**
- [ ] **Droplet isolation × 24 h** if meningococcus suspected; notify communicable disease
- [ ] O₂/airway support; treat seizures; ICU if ↓GCS or shock

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Admit (ICU if ↓GCS/shock/raised ICP) — Dx: Acute meningitis, organism pending
- [ ] Neuro obs + GCS q1–2h; vitals + SpO₂; head-up 30°; strict I/O
- [ ] **De-escalate to targeted therapy on culture/PCR**; duration — meningococcus 7 d, pneumococcus 10–14 d, Listeria ≥ 21 d
- [ ] Monitor & correct **hyponatraemia (SIADH)**; manage raised ICP; repeat imaging if deteriorating
- [ ] **[Thailand/HIV suspicion] TB meningitis → empiric anti-TB + dexamethasone taper 6–8 wk; cryptococcal → amphotericin B + flucytosine/fluconazole + therapeutic LPs**
- [ ] **Contact chemoprophylaxis** for close contacts of meningococcus: ciprofloxacin 500 mg PO single dose, or rifampicin 600 mg PO q12h × 2 d, or ceftriaxone 250 mg IM single
- [ ] Audiology follow-up (post-meningitic hearing loss); VTE prophylaxis per risk

> [!info]- Why
> Every hour of antibiotic delay worsens outcome, so cultures-then-treat-then-image is fixed. Dexamethasone only helps pneumococcal meningitis and only if given with/before antibiotics. Age > 50 / immunocompromise adds Listeria (β-lactam gap → ampicillin). Thailand's TB and cryptococcal meningitis need entirely different regimens, so they must be actively excluded. Verified vs IDSA / ESCMID 2016.
