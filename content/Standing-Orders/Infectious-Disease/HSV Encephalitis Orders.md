# HSV / Viral Encephalitis — ER Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local protocol and the attending. **Start empiric IV aciclovir 10 mg/kg q8h IMMEDIATELY** (before LP/PCR/MRI). **A negative early CSF HSV PCR does NOT exclude it** — continue + repeat. Hydrate + renally dose aciclovir. Consider Japanese encephalitis in Thailand.

## 🔴 One Day
- [ ] **Aciclovir 10 mg/kg IV q8h STAT** (renally dose-adjust) + **IV fluids** (crystal nephropathy prevention)
- [ ] **[If bacterial meningitis also possible] add empiric antibiotics ± dexamethasone** (see [[Bacterial Meningitis]]) until CSF/cultures clarify
- [ ] **CT head** if indicated (focal signs/↓GCS/seizure), then **LP**: CSF cell count/diff, protein, glucose, **HSV PCR** (+ VZV/enterovirus, JE/arbovirus per context), opening pressure
- [ ] **MRI brain** (temporal-lobe involvement); **EEG** (temporal PLEDs); bloods, cultures, **HIV test**
- [ ] **Treat seizures** (benzodiazepine → antiseizure medication); manage raised ICP/hyponatraemia

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Admit (neuro-ICU if ↓GCS/seizing) — Dx: encephalitis (?HSV/?JE/?autoimmune)
- [ ] **Continue aciclovir 14–21 days for HSV** (≥ 21 d if immunocompromised); **if early PCR negative but suspicion high → continue + repeat PCR in 3–7 days**; stop only if HSV excluded/alternative diagnosis
- [ ] Neuro obs + GCS; monitor renal function (aciclovir); correct hyponatraemia (SIADH)
- [ ] **Not improving/relapsing → consider anti-NMDAR autoimmune encephalitis** (immunotherapy)
- [ ] Neurorehabilitation planning; consider JE vaccination/public-health context

> [!info]- Why
> Untreated HSV encephalitis is devastating but aciclovir dramatically improves outcome, and every hour counts, so treatment starts empirically before confirmation; because early CSF PCR can be falsely negative, therapy continues with a repeat PCR. Aciclovir crystallises in the kidney without adequate hydration. Verified vs encephalitis guidance.
