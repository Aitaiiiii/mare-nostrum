# Severe Malaria — Admission Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; follow the current **Thai national malaria policy (CPG 2568)** and the attending. **Severe falciparum malaria → IV artesunate immediately.** Check G6PD before primaquine/tafenoquine; **never in pregnancy.** Watch hypoglycaemia + delayed post-artesunate haemolysis.

## 🔴 One Day
- [ ] **Thick + thin blood films** (species + % parasitaemia) STAT + **RDT**; repeat films q12–24h if negative but suspected
- [ ] Assess **severe-malaria criteria** (coma/seizure, respiratory distress, acidosis, AKI, jaundice, hypoglycaemia, severe anaemia, bleeding, shock, **Pf > 10%**)
- [ ] Labs: **glucose**, CBC, BUN/Cr, LFT, venous gas/lactate, coagulation, **G6PD**; blood culture (concurrent bacteraemia in severe)
- [ ] **[SEVERE] Artesunate 2.4 mg/kg IV bolus at 0, 12, 24 h then once daily** (**3 mg/kg if < 20 kg**) — continue ≥ 24 h until oral tolerated, then complete oral ACT
- [ ] **[Uncomplicated P. falciparum, Thailand] Dihydroartemisinin-piperaquine (oral) + single-dose primaquine 0.25 mg/kg** (artesunate-pyronaridine in Sisaket/Ubon)
- [ ] **[P. vivax / P. ovale] blood-stage drug (chloroquine/ACT) + primaquine 14 d (or tafenoquine) — only if G6PD normal, NEVER in pregnancy**
- [ ] **Treat hypoglycaemia** (frequent glucose); careful IV fluids (avoid overload/ARDS); transfuse severe anaemia
- [ ] Dose by ideal body weight (max 80 kg)

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Admit (ICU if cerebral/organ failure) — Dx: severe malaria (species ___, parasitaemia ___%)
- [ ] **Monitor parasitaemia every 12–24 h** until clearing; vitals + GCS; strict I/O
- [ ] Frequent glucose; manage AKI (dialysis if needed), acidosis, seizures, DIC as [[Sepsis (Sepsis-3)]]
- [ ] **Check Hb at 1–2 weeks — delayed post-artesunate haemolysis**
- [ ] Complete full oral ACT course; ensure hypnozoite eradication for vivax/ovale (after G6PD)
- [ ] Notify communicable disease; counsel prevention/relapse

> [!info]- Why
> IV artesunate is superior to quinine for severe malaria (SEAQUAMAT/AQUAMAT) and must not be delayed. Falciparum sequesters and causes organ failure; vivax/ovale relapse from liver hypnozoites need primaquine (G6PD-dependent). Hypoglycaemia is common and delayed haemolysis follows artesunate. Verified vs Thai malaria CPG 2568 + WHO.
