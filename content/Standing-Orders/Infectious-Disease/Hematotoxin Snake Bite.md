# Haematotoxic Snakebite — ER Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; follow local/QSMI protocol & **Ramathibodi Poison Center 1367**. Thai haematotoxic snakes: **Russell's viper, Malayan pit viper, green pit viper**. **NO tourniquet/incision/suction**; pressure-immobilisation is for elapids, not vipers (favour splint + immobilise). Watch for coagulopathy + AKI + local necrosis; always **exclude neurotoxicity** (Russell's viper can be mixed) — see [[Neurotoxic Snakebite]].

## 🔴 One Day
- [ ] First aid: immobilise + splint the limb, remove rings; **NO tourniquet, incision, or suction**; mark swelling margin + time
- [ ] **20-minute whole-blood clotting test (20-min WBCT)** at the bedside + **VCT, PT/INR/aPTT, fibrinogen, platelets, CBC, FDP/D-dimer**, renal function, CK, urinalysis (haematuria/myoglobin); blood group
- [ ] **Antivenom (QSMI polyvalent / green-pit-viper monovalent) — indications:** systemic bleeding, **incoagulable 20-min WBCT / VCT > 20 min / INR > 1.2**, platelets < 50k, or significant local envenoming (rapidly progressive swelling, threatened limb) — **have adrenaline, hydrocortisone, antihistamine ready for anaphylaxis**; repeat if coagulopathy persists at 6 h
- [ ] Analgesia (paracetamol/opioid — **avoid NSAIDs/IM injections** — bleeding risk); tetanus prophylaxis; wound care
- [ ] Monitor limb for **compartment syndrome** (fasciotomy only if measured pressure high — not for tense swelling alone); assess for **neurotoxic features** (ptosis — Russell's viper)

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Admit + observe ≥ 24 h — Dx: haematotoxic snakebite (species ___)
- [ ] **Repeat 20-min WBCT/coagulation at 6-hourly intervals**; give further antivenom for recurrent/persistent coagulopathy (venom outlasts a single dose)
- [ ] Monitor renal function/urine output (**AKI** — may need dialysis); manage bleeding (blood products only alongside antivenom if severe/surgery — antivenom is primary)
- [ ] Serial wound review (necrosis/infection → debridement, antibiotics only if infected); serum-sickness counselling after antivenom
- [ ] Discharge when coagulation normal + no progression; safety/prevention advice

> [!info]- Why
> Viper venom causes consumptive coagulopathy, bleeding, local necrosis, and AKI; antivenom (QSMI) is the specific treatment, given for coagulopathy/systemic bleeding/severe local envenoming and repeated because venom effects recur. Bedside 20-min WBCT guides both diagnosis and re-dosing. Tourniquets/incision worsen outcomes. Verified vs QSMI/Thai snakebite practice.
