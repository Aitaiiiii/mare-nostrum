# Pneumonia (CAP / HAP / Aspiration) — Admission Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local protocol, antibiogram, and the attending. Score severity first (**CURB-65**: Confusion / Urea > 7 / RR ≥ 30 / BP < 90/60 / age ≥ 65 → 0–1 home · 2 admit · ≥ 3 ICU). **Cover melioidosis (ceftazidime/meropenem) in a diabetic with severe CAP from endemic Thailand.** Septic shock → antibiotics within 1 h + [[Sepsis (Sepsis-3)]].

## 🔴 One Day
- [ ] Chest imaging: **CXR (PA + lateral)** — or lung ultrasound where available (2025 ATS)
- [ ] CBC, BUN/Cr, electrolytes, LFT, glucose; **procalcitonin** (baseline)
- [ ] **[Severe/inpatient] Blood cultures ×2 before antibiotics**, sputum Gram stain + C/S, **urine pneumococcal + Legionella antigen**, respiratory viral PCR (influenza, SARS-CoV-2)
- [ ] **[Thailand, severe] blood/sputum culture for melioidosis**; consider AFB/Xpert MTB if subacute/cavitary; HIV if risk
- [ ] ABG/VBG if SpO₂ < 92% or RR > 30
- [ ] O₂ to **SpO₂ ≥ 94%** (88–92% if COPD/CO₂ retainer); HFNC if moderate hypoxaemia
- [ ] **Empiric antibiotics within 4 h (1 h if shock):**
  - Outpatient (CURB 0–1, no comorbidity): **Amoxicillin 1 g PO tid** or **Doxycycline 100 mg PO bid**
  - Ward, non-severe: **Ceftriaxone 1–2 g IV OD + Azithromycin 500 mg IV/PO OD**, or **Levofloxacin 750 mg IV OD**
  - ICU, severe: **Ceftriaxone/Cefotaxime + Azithromycin (or Levofloxacin)** — not FQ monotherapy
  - MRSA risk (prior MRSA, post-influenza necrotising): **+ Vancomycin or Linezolid**
  - Pseudomonas risk (bronchiectasis/structural lung, prior Pseudomonas, recent broad ATB): **Piperacillin-tazobactam 4.5 g IV q6h** or **Cefepime 2 g IV q8h**
  - **Melioidosis (Thai, DM, severe):** **Ceftazidime 2 g IV q8h** or **Meropenem 1 g IV q8h**
  - Aspiration: **Ampicillin-sulbactam 3 g IV q6h** or **Piperacillin-tazobactam**
- [ ] **Oseltamivir 75 mg PO bid** if influenza suspected/confirmed
- [ ] **[Severe CAP] Hydrocortisone 200 mg/day IV** (50 mg q6h or infusion) days 1–4 then taper (CAPE COD) — not in non-severe/likely-influenza
- [ ] Paracetamol 500–1000 mg PO q6h PRN fever; IV fluid per hydration (avoid overload)

> [!info]- Why
> Right-drug-on-time and correct site of care drive mortality. Cultures before antibiotics preserve targeting; urine antigens catch pneumococcus/Legionella. Thailand's differentiator is melioidosis — standard β-lactams miss *B. pseudomallei*. Steroids help **severe** CAP only (CAPE COD). Verified vs ATS/IDSA 2019 + 2025 update.

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Admit Medicine (ICU if severe) — Dx: Pneumonia (CAP/HAP/aspiration), CURB-65 ___
- [ ] Condition; vitals + SpO₂ q4–8h (q1–2h if severe); strict I/O
- [ ] Continue empiric antibiotics; **de-escalate on culture data**; **IV → PO when afebrile + improving + tolerating oral (48–72 h)**
- [ ] **Duration:** stable outpatient/non-severe inpatient CAP **3–< 5 days**; severe CAP **≥ 5 days**; Legionella/complications 7–14 days; **melioidosis: intensive IV ≥ 10–14 days then oral eradication (co-trimoxazole) 3–6 months**
- [ ] Diet as tolerated; VTE prophylaxis per risk
- [ ] Procalcitonin trend to support stopping (fall ≥ 80% from peak or < 0.25)
- [ ] Before discharge: pneumococcal + influenza vaccination if due; smoking cessation
- [ ] **Follow-up CXR at 6 weeks** in smokers/older adults — confirm resolution, exclude malignancy

> [!info]- Why
> Short courses match modern evidence; longer therapy only for defined organisms/complications. IV-to-PO switch and de-escalation reduce length of stay and resistance. The 6-week film catches an obstructing tumour masquerading as pneumonia.
