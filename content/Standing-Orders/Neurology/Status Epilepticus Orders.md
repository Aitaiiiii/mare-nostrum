# Status Epilepticus — ER Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local protocol and the attending. **Treat at 5 min with a FULL-dose benzodiazepine — under-dosing drives refractoriness.** Check glucose (thiamine before glucose if alcoholic/malnourished). Not waking after seizures → EEG (non-convulsive SE).

## 🔴 One Day (timed protocol)
- [ ] **0–5 min:** ABC, high-flow O₂, IV access, monitor; **capillary glucose** → treat hypoglycaemia (thiamine 100 mg IV first if alcoholic/malnourished)
- [ ] **5–20 min — benzodiazepine, full dose:** **Lorazepam 0.1 mg/kg IV (max 4 mg), repeat ×1** — OR **Midazolam 10 mg IM (no IV access)** — OR **Diazepam 0.15–0.2 mg/kg IV (max 10 mg), repeat ×1**
- [ ] **20–40 min — second-line IV ASM (ESETT-equivalent, choose one):** **Levetiracetam 60 mg/kg (max 4.5 g)** · **Valproate 40 mg/kg (max 3 g; avoid hepatic failure/pregnancy)** · **Fosphenytoin 20 mg PE/kg (cardiac monitor)**
- [ ] **> 40 min — refractory SE:** intubate + **anaesthetic infusion (midazolam / propofol / pentobarbital-thiopental)** titrated to seizure suppression on **continuous EEG**; ICU
- [ ] Labs: glucose, electrolytes (Na/Ca/Mg), BUN/Cr, LFT, **ASM levels**, CK, ABG, toxicology, **pregnancy test**
- [ ] Cause-specific: **eclampsia → MgSO₄** · **isoniazid → pyridoxine** · hyponatraemia → hypertonic saline · CNS infection → empiric antimicrobials + LP
- [ ] CT head once stabilised; LP if CNS infection suspected; **EEG if not waking** (non-convulsive SE)

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Admit (ICU if refractory/intubated) — Dx: status epilepticus, ?cause
- [ ] Continuous EEG for refractory/non-convulsive SE; wean anaesthetic slowly with EEG guidance
- [ ] **Start/optimise maintenance antiseizure medication**; restore levels in known epilepsy
- [ ] Treat systemic effects: hyperthermia, **rhabdomyolysis** (CK, fluids), aspiration, acidosis
- [ ] Treat the precipitant; neurology follow-up; medication-adherence counselling

> [!info]- Why
> Seizures rarely stop after 5 min and GABA-A receptors internalise over time (benzodiazepine resistance), so speed and full dosing matter most; ESETT showed levetiracetam, valproate and fosphenytoin are equivalent second-line. Refractory SE needs anaesthesia + cEEG, not endless boluses. Glucose/thiamine, eclampsia (Mg) and INH (pyridoxine) are reversible causes. Verified vs AES 2016 + ESETT.
