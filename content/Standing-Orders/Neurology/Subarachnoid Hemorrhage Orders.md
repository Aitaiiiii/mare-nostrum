# Aneurysmal SAH — ER/Neuro-ICU Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local protocol and the attending. **Thunderclap headache = SAH until excluded** (CT → LP xanthochromia if CT negative). **Re-bleed highest in first 24 h → control BP + secure aneurysm < 24 h.** Nimodipine is for DCI outcome, not the bleed. Do NOT fluid-restrict.

## 🔴 One Day
- [ ] **Non-contrast CT head STAT** (~100% sensitive < 6 h); if negative + persistent suspicion → **LP for xanthochromia** (≥ 6–12 h from onset)
- [ ] Once SAH confirmed → **CT/catheter angiography** to define the aneurysm; **urgent neurosurgery/neuro-IR referral**
- [ ] **Secure aneurysm within 24 h — coiling or clipping** (transfer to neurocritical-care centre)
- [ ] **BP control pre-securing: SBP < ~160 mmHg** with titratable IV agent (nicardipine/labetalol); avoid hypotension/swings
- [ ] **Nimodipine 60 mg PO/NG q4h** (start early; hold/reduce for hypotension) — continue 21 days
- [ ] Analgesia + antiemetic; bed rest, head-up; treat seizures (ASM 7 days for new-onset seizure)
- [ ] Labs: CBC, coagulation, electrolytes (Na⁺ baseline), glucose, troponin, ECG; group & save
- [ ] **EVD for hydrocephalus** (declining consciousness) — neurosurgery

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Admit neuro-ICU — Dx: aneurysmal SAH (Hunt-Hess ___, modified Fisher ___)
- [ ] Neuro obs + GCS + pupils hourly; monitor for **re-bleed, hydrocephalus, DCI**
- [ ] **Nimodipine 60 mg q4h to complete 21 days**
- [ ] **DCI/vasospasm watch days 4–14** → maintain **euvolaemia + induced hypertension** (once secured); TCD/CTA monitoring; endovascular rescue (IA vasodilator/angioplasty) for refractory DCI
- [ ] **Monitor Na⁺ — cerebral salt wasting/SIADH**; correct with isotonic/hypertonic saline, **do NOT fluid-restrict** a hypovolaemic SAH patient
- [ ] Normoglycaemia, normothermia, DVT prophylaxis (IPC pre-securing; pharmacological after), bowel/stress care
- [ ] Manage neurogenic cardiac/pulmonary effects (stunned myocardium, neurogenic pulmonary oedema)

> [!info]- Why
> The lethal early event is re-rupture (peak < 24 h), so BP control + rapid aneurysm securing dominate day 1; nimodipine improves outcome from delayed cerebral ischaemia at days 4–14, managed by euvolaemia + induced hypertension rather than fluid restriction. EVD relieves obstructive hydrocephalus. Verified vs 2023 AHA/ASA aSAH.
