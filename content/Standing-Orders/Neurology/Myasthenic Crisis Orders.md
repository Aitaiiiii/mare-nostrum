# Myasthenic Crisis — Admission Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local protocol and the attending. **Intubate EARLY on FVC/NIF trends + bulbar failure — hypoxaemia/hypercapnia are LATE.** **Avoid MG-worsening drugs** (aminoglycosides, fluoroquinolones, macrolides, β-blockers, IV magnesium, NMBs). Steroids can transiently worsen weakness.

## 🔴 One Day
- [ ] **Serial FVC + NIF/MIP** (single-breath count surrogate) — **elective intubation** for declining mechanics/bulbar failure/secretions (don't wait for ABG)
- [ ] Continuous SpO₂/cardiac monitoring; assess swallow (aspiration → NPO if unsafe)
- [ ] **Immunotherapy — choose one: IVIG 0.4 g/kg/day IV × 5 days OR plasma exchange (~5 sessions, 1–1.5 plasma volumes every other day)**
- [ ] **Find + treat the precipitant** (septic screen, CXR, cultures — infection commonest)
- [ ] **Avoid**: aminoglycosides, fluoroquinolones, macrolides, β-blockers, **IV magnesium**, neuromuscular blockers, procainamide/quinine
- [ ] Manage pyridostigmine (often held while intubated — secretions; restart on recovery); exclude cholinergic crisis
- [ ] Neurology + ICU referral

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Admit ICU/HDU — Dx: myasthenic crisis (precipitant ___)
- [ ] FVC/NIF monitoring while weak; ventilatory support/weaning per mechanics
- [ ] Corticosteroids for longer-term control — **start under IVIG/PLEX + ventilatory cover** (can transiently worsen weakness 1–2 wk)
- [ ] VTE prophylaxis; nutrition (aspiration precautions/NG); physiotherapy
- [ ] Thymoma assessment/thymectomy planning after recovery; neurology follow-up + steroid-sparing agents

> [!info]- Why
> Death is from respiratory failure, and gas exchange fails late, so early intubation guided by FVC/NIF is the life-saver; IVIG and plasma exchange are equally effective fast-acting therapies. Many common antibiotics and IV magnesium worsen neuromuscular transmission and must be avoided, and steroids can transiently deepen weakness before helping. Verified vs MG-crisis evidence.
