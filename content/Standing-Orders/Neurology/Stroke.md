# Acute Ischemic Stroke — ER Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local protocol and the attending. **Time is brain — activate code stroke.** **Non-contrast CT first to exclude haemorrhage** (→ [[Intracerebral Hemorrhage (ICH)]] if bleed). **Check glucose** (hypoglycaemia mimics stroke). Thrombolysis window ≤ 4.5 h; large-vessel occlusion thrombectomy ≤ 24 h (selected). Do NOT lower BP unless > 185/110 (pre-lysis) or > 220/120.

## 🔴 One Day
- [ ] **Code stroke**: last-known-well time, NIHSS, **capillary glucose** (treat hypoglycaemia), airway/O₂ (target SpO₂ ≥ 94%)
- [ ] **STAT non-contrast CT head ± CT angiography/perfusion** — exclude haemorrhage, identify large-vessel occlusion (LVO)
- [ ] Labs: glucose, electrolytes, CBC, **coagulation/INR**, troponin; ECG (AF)
- [ ] **IV thrombolysis (alteplase 0.9 mg/kg or tenecteplase 0.25 mg/kg) if within 4.5 h + no contraindication** — **BP must be < 185/110 before and < 180/105 for 24 h after** (labetalol/nicardipine to achieve)
- [ ] **Mechanical thrombectomy for LVO** (up to 24 h in selected patients by perfusion/ASPECTS) → activate neuro-intervention/transfer
- [ ] **BP: do NOT treat unless > 220/120** (no lysis/thrombectomy), or the thresholds above if reperfusion therapy planned
- [ ] NPO until **swallow screen** passed (aspiration prevention)

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Admit stroke unit — Dx: acute ischaemic stroke (territory ___, NIHSS ___, ± reperfusion therapy)
- [ ] Neuro obs + NIHSS; post-lysis BP control + no antithrombotics/lines for 24 h; **repeat CT at 24 h** before starting antiplatelet (post-lysis)
- [ ] **Antiplatelet: aspirin** (start 24 h after lysis, or immediately if no lysis); **DAPT (aspirin + clopidogrel 21 days) for minor stroke/high-risk TIA**
- [ ] **High-intensity statin**; glucose control (avoid hyper/hypoglycaemia); normothermia; VTE prophylaxis (IPC first, pharmacological later); early swallow/mobilisation/rehab
- [ ] **Secondary prevention work-up:** carotid imaging, echocardiogram, **AF monitoring → anticoagulation for cardioembolic** (timing by infarct size)

> [!info]- Why
> Reperfusion (thrombolysis ≤ 4.5 h, thrombectomy for LVO ≤ 24 h) salvages penumbra, so the pathway is CT-to-exclude-bleed → treat fast; BP is left high to maintain penumbral perfusion except when lysis/thrombectomy requires < 185/110. Glucose and swallow screening prevent avoidable harm. Verified vs AHA/ASA AIS guidance.
