# Alcohol Withdrawal & DTs — ER/Admission Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local protocol and the attending. **Give thiamine BEFORE any glucose** (Wernicke risk). **DTs = emergency** → front-loaded benzodiazepines + ICU. Exclude head injury/subdural, sepsis, hepatic encephalopathy, hypoglycaemia.

## 🔴 One Day
- [ ] **Thiamine 100 mg IV/IM STAT — BEFORE glucose-containing fluid** (Wernicke suspected → **500 mg IV q8h × 72 h**)
- [ ] Capillary glucose; labs: electrolytes, **magnesium, phosphate**, Ca, BUN/Cr, LFT, CBC, coagulation; ECG
- [ ] Consider CT head (trauma/focal signs/seizure/not improving), septic screen
- [ ] **CIWA-Ar scoring** q1–2h to guide dosing
- [ ] **Benzodiazepine — symptom-triggered by CIWA-Ar** (e.g. diazepam 10–20 mg PO/IV or chlordiazepoxide when CIWA ≥ 8–10; **lorazepam 2–4 mg if significant liver disease/elderly**)
- [ ] **[Severe / DTs] Front-loaded escalating IV benzodiazepine** (e.g. diazepam 10–20 mg IV q5–10min, no ceiling) titrated to light sedation
- [ ] Replace **magnesium** (MgSO₄) and potassium/phosphate as needed
- [ ] IV fluids; treat precipitant/comorbidity

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Admit (ICU if DTs/refractory) — Dx: alcohol withdrawal syndrome / DTs
- [ ] Continue CIWA-Ar–triggered benzodiazepine; long-acting agent gives a self-tapering course
- [ ] **[Refractory DTs] Phenobarbital (experienced protocol) or benzodiazepine infusion; ICU; dexmedetomidine/propofol as ADJUNCTS only** (dexmedetomidine does not prevent seizures — never sole agent)
- [ ] Vitals + neuro obs; calm low-stimulus environment; fall/aspiration precautions
- [ ] Continue thiamine + electrolyte replacement; monitor Mg/K/PO₄
- [ ] Withdrawal seizures → benzodiazepine (no chronic anticonvulsant for uncomplicated withdrawal seizures)
- [ ] Addiction/psychiatric referral; nutrition; discharge planning

> [!info]- Why
> Benzodiazepines replace the GABA deficit that drives withdrawal — first-line for all stages; symptom-triggered dosing minimises total drug in stable patients while front-loading controls DTs. Thiamine precedes glucose because a glucose load consumes residual thiamine and can trigger Wernicke. Low magnesium potentiates seizures and impairs thiamine use. Verified vs standard AWS/DTs management + CIWA-Ar evidence.
