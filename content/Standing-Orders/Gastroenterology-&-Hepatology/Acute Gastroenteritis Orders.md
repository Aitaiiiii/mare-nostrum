# Acute Gastroenteritis / Infectious Diarrhea — ER Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local protocol and the attending. **Rehydration is the treatment; most cases need NO antibiotics.** Do NOT give antibiotics if EHEC/STEC suspected (bloody diarrhoea/outbreak → HUS risk). No loperamide in dysentery / *C. difficile*.

## 🔴 One Day
- [ ] **Grade dehydration** (vitals, mucosa, turgor, urine output, mental status)
- [ ] **Mild–moderate → ORS (low-osmolarity WHO)**, frequent small sips; replace ongoing losses
- [ ] **Severe/shock or can't tolerate oral → IV isotonic crystalloid** (rapid bolus to restore perfusion, then deficit + losses); correct **K⁺** and acidosis
- [ ] **Continue feeding** (do not fast)
- [ ] Labs only if severe/dehydrated/dysenteric/immunocompromised: electrolytes, BUN/Cr, VBG; **stool exam + culture, faecal leukocytes, O&P (Entamoeba)**; **C. difficile toxin/PCR** if recent antibiotics; blood cultures if septic; **β-hCG** where relevant
- [ ] **Antibiotics ONLY if indicated:**
  - Dysentery/severe/immunocompromised/cholera → **Azithromycin 500 mg PO OD × 3 d** (preferred where Campylobacter/FQ resistance high) or **Ciprofloxacin 500 mg PO bid**
  - Amoebic dysentery → **Metronidazole 750 mg PO tid × 7–10 d + luminal agent** (diloxanide/paromomycin)
  - **Suspected STEC/O157 → NO antibiotics** (HUS risk)
- [ ] Antiemetic (ondansetron) to aid oral rehydration; **zinc** in children
- [ ] **Avoid loperamide** in dysentery / *C. difficile* / suspected invasive infection

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Admit if severe dehydration, unable to maintain oral intake, AKI, sepsis, or high-risk host — else discharge with ORS + return precautions
- [ ] Vitals + I/O; monitor stool frequency/character, urine output, electrolytes, renal function
- [ ] Continue ORS/IV until rehydrated and oral intake adequate; de-escalate/stop antibiotics per culture
- [ ] Watch for complications: HUS (STEC), toxic megacolon, bacteraemia (immunocompromised), persistent/dysenteric course → re-evaluate
- [ ] Hand-hygiene/isolation as indicated; public-health notification for outbreak/cholera; dietary advice

> [!info]- Why
> Death and morbidity come from dehydration/AKI, not the organism — ORS (effective even in cholera) and IV fluids are the core therapy. Most acute diarrhoea is viral/toxin-mediated and self-limiting, so antibiotics are the exception; in STEC they precipitate HUS, and antimotility agents in invasive/dysenteric disease risk toxic megacolon. Verified vs IDSA/WHO acute-diarrhoea principles.
