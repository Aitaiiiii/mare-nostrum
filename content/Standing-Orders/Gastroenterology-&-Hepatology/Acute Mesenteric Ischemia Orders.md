# Acute Mesenteric Ischemia — ER Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local protocol and the attending. **Pain out of proportion to exam = AMI → CT angiography now + surgery/vascular referral.** A normal lactate does not exclude it. Peritonism/shock = infarction → emergency laparotomy.

## 🔴 One Day
- [ ] **Multiphasic (arterial + portal-venous) CT angiography STAT** — do not delay for observation
- [ ] Labs: **lactate**, VBG/ABG, CBC, coagulation, U&E/creatinine, LFT, amylase/lipase, group & crossmatch; ECG (AF/MI)
- [ ] **IV fluid resuscitation** (large third-space losses) — crystalloid to perfusion targets
- [ ] **IV unfractionated heparin** (unless active bleeding/contraindication)
- [ ] **Broad-spectrum antibiotics** (Gram-negative + anaerobic, e.g. piperacillin-tazobactam or carbapenem ± metronidazole)
- [ ] **NG tube decompression**; analgesia (opioid); antiemetic; NPO
- [ ] Correct acidosis/electrolytes; urinary catheter + strict I/O
- [ ] **Urgent surgery + vascular/IR referral** — type-directed:
  - SMA embolism → embolectomy (or endovascular) + laparotomy/resect non-viable bowel
  - SMA thrombosis → revascularisation (stent/bypass) + resection
  - NOMI → optimise cardiac output/stop vasopressors, **intra-arterial papaverine**, resect necrosis
  - Venous (MVT) → **anticoagulation primary**, resect only if infarcted

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Admit surgery/ICU — Dx: AMI (type ___)
- [ ] Continue heparin + antibiotics; serial abdominal exam + lactate/acid-base trend
- [ ] **Planned second-look laparotomy at 24–48 h** for marginal bowel (preserve length)
- [ ] ICU support for sepsis/reperfusion physiology; monitor for short-bowel after resection
- [ ] Identify source: echo (AF/thrombus) → long-term anticoagulation for embolic; thrombophilia/malignancy work-up for venous; atherosclerosis risk management for thrombotic

> [!info]- Why
> Bowel dies fast, so the bundle is resuscitate + heparin + broad antibiotics (translocation) + urgent revascularisation, with resection of dead bowel and a second look to save borderline segments. CTA confirms and localises; lactate/peritonism come too late to wait for. Type dictates the intervention (NOMI = perfusion, venous = anticoagulation). Verified vs WSES AMI.
