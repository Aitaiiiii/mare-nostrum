# Bowel Obstruction — ER/Admission Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local protocol and the attending. **Strangulation/ischaemia (continuous pain, fever, tachycardia, peritonism, rising lactate) → emergency surgery — do NOT persist with conservative care.** Examine all hernial orifices + rectum. Closed-loop/complete obstruction/obstructed hernia → surgery.

## 🔴 One Day
- [ ] **NPO**; **NG tube on free drainage** (decompress); IV access
- [ ] **IV fluid resuscitation + correct electrolytes (K⁺!)**; urinary catheter + strict I/O
- [ ] **CT abdomen/pelvis with contrast** (transition point, cause, **ischaemia signs**); erect/supine AXR as quick screen (limits 3/6/9 cm)
- [ ] Labs: CBC, U&E, **lactate**, CRP, amylase, coagulation, **group & save**
- [ ] Analgesia + antiemetic; VTE prophylaxis
- [ ] **Surgical referral**; decide: conservative trial vs operation
  - **Strangulation/ischaemia/peritonitis/closed loop/complete/obstructed hernia → emergency laparotomy**
  - **Sigmoid volvulus → endoscopic decompression/detorsion** (surgery if failed/ischaemic)
  - **LBO from cancer → resection ± stoma / stenting bridge**
- [ ] Water-soluble contrast (Gastrografin) study for uncomplicated adhesive SBO (prognostic ± therapeutic)

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Admit surgery — Dx: bowel obstruction (SBO/LBO, cause ___)
- [ ] **Uncomplicated partial adhesive SBO → conservative "drip and suck" trial 24–48 h**; reassess exam + lactate frequently
- [ ] **Failure to progress or any deterioration → surgery**
- [ ] Serial abdominal exam, NG output, electrolytes, lactate; monitor for strangulation
- [ ] Ogilvie's (pseudo-obstruction) → treat precipitant, correct electrolytes, decompress, **neostigmine** if refractory (monitored)
- [ ] Post-op / recovery care; nutrition; treat underlying cause (cancer staging etc.)

> [!info]- Why
> Most partial adhesive SBO resolve with decompression and fluids, but strangulation/closed-loop obstruction ischaemias fast and needs the knife — so frequent reassessment and lactate/peritonism are the triggers to operate. NG + IV fluids relieve distension and correct the vomiting-related losses. LBO and volvulus have their own pathways. Verified vs WSES adhesive-SBO principles.
