# Postpartum Hemorrhage — Emergency Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local obstetric protocol. **Call for help + activate massive transfusion early** (visual estimation underestimates loss). **Give tranexamic acid within 3 h.** Ergometrine — avoid in HTN/pre-eclampsia; carboprost — avoid in asthma.

## 🔴 One Day
- [ ] Call for help; 2 large-bore IVs; **crossmatch / activate massive-transfusion protocol**; O₂; warm patient; catheterise (empty bladder)
- [ ] Bloods: CBC, **coagulation + fibrinogen**, group & save/crossmatch
- [ ] **Identify cause (4 Ts): Tone (boggy uterus) · Trauma (inspect tract) · Tissue (placenta complete?) · Thrombin (coagulopathy)**
- [ ] **Uterine massage / bimanual compression**
- [ ] **Oxytocin (1st-line uterotonic)** IV infusion/IM
- [ ] Escalate uterotonics: **Ergometrine IM** (not in HTN/PET), **Carboprost IM** (not in asthma), **Misoprostol 800 µg SL/PR**
- [ ] **Tranexamic acid 1 g IV as soon as PPH diagnosed (within 3 h)** — repeat once if bleeding continues
- [ ] Repair lacerations; remove retained placenta/products; correct coagulopathy (FFP/platelets/cryo/fibrinogen); reduce uterine inversion
- [ ] Ongoing bleeding → **intrauterine balloon tamponade (Bakri)** → theatre

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Transfer to theatre/HDU/ICU as needed — Dx: PPH (cause ___)
- [ ] Surgery if refractory: **B-Lynch compression suture, uterine/internal iliac ligation, uterine artery embolisation, hysterectomy (last resort)**
- [ ] Ongoing resuscitation guided by vitals + labs/viscoelastic testing; monitor urine output; DVT prophylaxis once bleeding controlled
- [ ] Debrief, anaemia management, Anti-D if indicated

> [!info]- Why
> Atony causes ~70% of PPH, so massage + oxytocin + escalating uterotonics come first, with early tranexamic acid reducing death from bleeding (WOMAN). Young mothers compensate then crash, so resuscitate and activate blood early. Refractory bleeding escalates promptly to mechanical/surgical control. Verified vs WHO/FIGO PPH.
