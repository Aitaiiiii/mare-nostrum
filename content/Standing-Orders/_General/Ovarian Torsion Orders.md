# Ovarian Torsion — ER Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local protocol and the attending. **Sudden severe unilateral pelvic pain ± adnexal mass = torsion until excluded → urgent gynaecology/surgery.** A normal Doppler does NOT exclude it. β-hCG in every woman. Detorse and CONSERVE the ovary.

## 🔴 One Day
- [ ] **β-hCG** (exclude ectopic); CBC, urinalysis, group & save
- [ ] **Transvaginal/transabdominal US with Doppler** (enlarged ovary, whirlpool sign, ± flow) — **present flow does not exclude torsion**
- [ ] Analgesia (opioid) + antiemetic; **NPO**; IV fluids
- [ ] **Urgent gynaecology/surgery referral — laparoscopy for detorsion** (do NOT delay for imaging when suspicion high)
- [ ] Consent/prepare for theatre

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Admit gynaecology — Dx: ovarian/adnexal torsion (side ___)
- [ ] **Detorse + conserve the ovary** (even if dusky/ischaemic); cystectomy of causative cyst; **oophoropexy** if recurrent; oophorectomy only if necrotic/malignant
- [ ] Post-op care; histology if mass; follow-up (fertility counselling)

> [!info]- Why
> The ovary infarcts with time but recovers even after prolonged torsion (dual supply), so surgery is expedited on clinical suspicion and the ovary is conserved rather than removed; Doppler is supportive but cannot exclude torsion. β-hCG excludes the ectopic mimic. Verified vs gynaecological practice.
