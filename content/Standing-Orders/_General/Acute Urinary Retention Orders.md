# Acute Urinary Retention — ER Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local protocol and the attending. **Retention + saddle anaesthesia / bilateral leg signs / back pain = cauda equina → urgent MRI + neurosurgery.** Record the drained volume; watch for post-obstructive diuresis and obstructive AKI.

## 🔴 One Day
- [ ] **Immediate urethral catheterisation** (suprapubic if urethral fails/contraindicated) — **record residual volume**; analgesia
- [ ] Bladder scan/US to confirm; **PR exam** (prostate/faecal impaction/anal tone) + **perineal sensation + lower-limb neurology**
- [ ] Labs: **urinalysis + culture, renal function/electrolytes**, glucose; renal US if renal impairment; **defer PSA** (falsely raised in retention)
- [ ] **[Neurological features — saddle anaesthesia/back pain/leg signs] urgent MRI spine (cauda equina)** → neurosurgery (see [[Spinal Cord Compression & Cauda Equina]])
- [ ] Treat precipitants: UTI/prostatitis (antibiotics), constipation, stop offending drugs (anticholinergics/opioids)
- [ ] **[Likely BPH, male] start α-blocker (tamsulosin)**

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Admit or discharge with catheter + plan per volume/cause — Dx: acute urinary retention (cause ___)
- [ ] **Monitor for post-obstructive diuresis** after large-volume decompression → urine output, fluid balance, weight, electrolytes; **replace fluids/electrolytes if pathological**
- [ ] Manage obstructive AKI ([[Acute Kidney Injury]])
- [ ] **Trial without catheter (TWOC)** after α-blocker; failed TWOC → urology (definitive BPH management)
- [ ] Urology follow-up

> [!info]- Why
> Catheterisation relieves the bladder immediately, but the retained-volume record, cause search, and monitoring for post-obstructive diuresis/AKI complete safe care; an α-blocker before TWOC improves the chance of voiding. The critical miss is a neurological (cauda equina) cause, which needs urgent MRI, not just a catheter. Verified vs urological practice.
