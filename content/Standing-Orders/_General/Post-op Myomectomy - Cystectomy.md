# Post-op Myomectomy / Cystectomy (Laparotomy) — Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local protocol and the attending. **Myomectomy can bleed heavily — monitor for concealed intra-abdominal haemorrhage (tachycardia, distension, falling Hct) and check post-op Hb.** The myometrial bed carries hematoma + infection risk (rationale for a full IV antibiotic course by unit practice). **If the uterine cavity was entered, counsel for elective C-S in a future pregnancy** (rupture risk). Send all specimens for histopathology.

## 🔴 One Day (post-op)
- [ ] Note number/size of myomas + **whether the endometrial cavity was entered** (→ future C-S advice); EBL + pre/post-op Hb
- [ ] Routine post-op care; **observe bleeding per vagina + wound + abdominal signs**; **send specimen for histopathology**
- [ ] **IV: Acetar 1000 mL IV at 120 mL/h ×2 → 5%D-N/2 1000 mL IV at 120 mL/h**; **NPO; retain Foley catheter**
- [ ] **Morphine 3 mg IV q6h PRN** for pain; **metoclopramide 10 mg IV q8h PRN** for nausea

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Record vital signs + I/O; **POD 2:** encourage ambulation; **POD 3:** open + dress wound, discharge planning, wound review day 7, follow-up OPD Gynae + histology
- [ ] **Antibiotic (per attending): ceftriaxone 2 g IV OD + metronidazole 500 mg IV q8h ×3 days → step down to amoxicillin-clavulanate 1 g PO bid #14** (myometrial bed = hematoma/infection risk; a single pre-incision dose is the evidence minimum, extended course is unit practice)
- [ ] **Paracetamol 500 mg PO q4–6h PRN** (#20) + **ibuprofen 400 mg PO tid** (#20) — multimodal analgesia
- [ ] **Simethicone 80 mg PO tid** (#20) for wind; continue home meds (e.g. amlodipine 5 mg PO bid if pre-existing)
- [ ] Advance diet as bowel function returns; **VTE prophylaxis + early mobilization** (laparotomy); monitor for ileus, wound infection, bleeding

> [!info]- Why
> Open myomectomy/cystectomy is a laparotomy whose specific hazards are heavy blood loss and a raw myometrial bed prone to hematoma/infection, so orders emphasize haemorrhage surveillance, a fuller antibiotic course (unit practice), staged wound care, and — after cavity entry — counselling on future delivery mode. Otherwise standard ERAS/VTE laparotomy principles apply. Verified vs gynaecology laparotomy + ERAS standards.
