# Post-op Lower-Transverse C-S — Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local protocol and the attending. **Uterine atony is the leading cause of post-C-S PPH — monitor uterine tone + lochia closely in the first hours; keep the oxytocin infusion running.** Escalate bleeding → [[Postpartum Hemorrhage (PPH)]]. **C-S carries higher VTE risk than vaginal delivery — early mobilization + VTE prophylaxis for high-risk women.** Watch for post-spinal N/V + shivering and post-op ileus (flatus/bowel sounds before solids).

## 🔴 One Day (immediate post-op)
- [ ] **Vital signs q15 min ×4 → q30 min ×2 → q1h until stable → q4h**; **observe uterine tone + lochia (bleeding per vagina)**; wound check; **retain Foley + record I/O**
- [ ] **IV: Acetar 1000 mL + oxytocin (Syntocinon) 20 IU IV at 120 mL/h ×2 bags → then 5%D-N/2 1000 mL IV at 120 mL/h** (uterotonic infusion — atony prevention)
- [ ] **Pain control per anaesthesia order** (neuraxial morphine / multimodal); **morphine 3 mg IV q6h PRN** for breakthrough pain
- [ ] **Metoclopramide 10 mg IV q8h PRN** for nausea/vomiting
- [ ] Diet: cold sips → **soft diet next morning** as tolerated; when IV finished → **off IV + remove Foley** (≥ 12 h post-op, once mobile)

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Record vital signs + I/O; **wound check daily**; **observe uterine tone + lochia**
- [ ] **Antibiotic (per attending): ceftriaxone 2 g IV OD + metronidazole 500 mg IV q8h** (many units use cefazolin 1 g IV q6h ×4 → oral amoxicillin-clavulanate 1 g PO bid #14, or oral dicloxacillin) — a single pre-incision cefazolin dose is the evidence-based minimum; extended courses are unit practice
- [ ] **Paracetamol 500–1000 mg PO q4–6h PRN** (#20) + **ibuprofen 400 mg PO tid** (#20) — multimodal analgesia
- [ ] **Simethicone 80 mg PO tid** (#20) for wind pain; **ferrous + folate (Triferdine) 1 PO OD** (#60) + **calcium carbonate 1 PO OD** (#60) — postpartum supplements
- [ ] Promote **breastfeeding + early ambulation**; monitor for uterine tone/lochia, endometritis, wound infection, DVT
- [ ] **Pre-eclampsia / overt DM → manage on the pre-eclampsia / DM pathway** (MgSO₄ / insulin) — see [[Pre-eclampsia]] · [[GDMA - DM in Pregnancy]]
- [ ] Discharge: usually ~72 h, mother + infant stable, feeding established → follow-up postpartum clinic 6 weeks + family planning; continue home meds

> [!info]- Why
> The early post-C-S priorities are preventing atonic PPH (continued uterotonic + tone/lochia monitoring), preventing infection (timed prophylaxis — a single pre-incision dose is what the evidence supports, though many Thai units extend), controlling pain multimodally to enable early feeding/mobilization, and mitigating the raised VTE risk. Verified vs ACOG/WHO caesarean + postpartum standards.
