# Pre-eclampsia / Eclampsia — Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local obstetric protocol and the attending. **Magnesium sulfate for severe features/eclampsia** (monitor toxicity — calcium gluconate antidote). **Treat severe hypertension (≥ 160/110) within 30–60 min** to prevent stroke. **Delivery is the only cure.** **Eclampsia can occur postpartum.** Restrict fluids (pulmonary oedema).

## 🔴 One Day
- [ ] Confirm: new HTN ≥ 140/90 after 20 wk + proteinuria/end-organ features; identify **severe features** (BP ≥ 160/110, HELLP, ↑LFT, thrombocytopenia < 100k, creatinine, pulmonary oedema, cerebral/visual symptoms)
- [ ] Labs: CBC (platelets, haemolysis film/LDH/haptoglobin), **LFT (AST/ALT), renal function/uric acid, urine protein:creatinine**, coagulation; **fetal assessment (CTG, ultrasound/Doppler)**; group & save
- [ ] **Magnesium sulfate (severe features / eclampsia):** **Zuspan — 4–6 g IV over 15–20 min then 1–2 g/h infusion** (or Pritchard IM), continue **24 h after delivery or last seizure**; **monitor reflexes, RR, urine output, SpO₂; antidote = calcium gluconate 1 g IV**
- [ ] **Severe hypertension (≥ 160/110) → treat within 30–60 min:** **labetalol IV / hydralazine IV / oral immediate-release nifedipine**, aim ~140/90 (not lower)
- [ ] **Fluid restriction** (~80 mL/h — pulmonary oedema risk); urinary catheter + strict I/O; **antenatal corticosteroids if < 34 wk** (fetal lungs) when time permits
- [ ] Notify obstetrics/anaesthetics/neonatology

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Admit — Dx: pre-eclampsia with/without severe features / eclampsia
- [ ] **Delivery — the only cure:** ≥ 37 wk (no severe features); **≥ 34 wk (or sooner/now) for severe features/eclampsia/HELLP/maternal-fetal compromise** after stabilisation
- [ ] Continue MgSO₄ + BP control **through 24 h postpartum**; monitor for HELLP/DIC/abruption/AKI/pulmonary oedema
- [ ] **Postpartum vigilance** (eclampsia can occur up to ~6 weeks); transition to oral antihypertensive; counsel future CVD/recurrence risk

> [!info]- Why
> Magnesium sulfate prevents/treats eclamptic seizures (superior to standard anticonvulsants) and severe hypertension must be lowered promptly to prevent maternal stroke, while the definitive treatment is delivery of the placenta. Fluid restriction avoids pulmonary oedema, and postpartum eclampsia awareness matters. Verified vs ACOG (Practice Bulletin 222) + Thai practice.
