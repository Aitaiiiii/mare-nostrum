# Major Burns — ER Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local burn-unit protocol. **Airway first — intubate EARLY for inhalation injury** (facial burns/soot/hoarseness/enclosed space) before oedema. Titrate Parkland to urine output (avoid fluid creep). Keep the patient warm. Refer major burns to a burn centre.

## 🔴 One Day
- [ ] Primary survey (ATLS); stop the burning; remove clothing/jewellery; high-flow O₂
- [ ] **Airway: inhalation injury signs → early elective intubation**; suspect **CO/cyanide** in enclosed-space fires (see [[Carbon Monoxide Poisoning]])
- [ ] Estimate **depth + %TBSA (Rule of Nines; palm ≈ 1%; Lund-Browder in children)** — count partial + full thickness
- [ ] **[≥ 20% TBSA adult / ≥ 10% child] Parkland: Lactated Ringer's 4 mL/kg/%TBSA over 24 h (3 mL child), HALF in first 8 h from injury** — **titrate to urine output ~0.5 mL/kg/h** (higher if electrical/pigmenturia)
- [ ] Large-bore IV (through burned skin if needed); **urinary catheter** (monitor output); NG tube for large burns
- [ ] Labs: CBC, U&E, venous gas ± carboxyhaemoglobin, CK, group & save, glucose
- [ ] **Strong IV analgesia (opioid)**; **tetanus prophylaxis**; keep warm; clean dressings; topical antimicrobial (no prophylactic systemic antibiotics)
- [ ] **Circumferential full-thickness limb/chest burn → escharotomy**

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Admit (burn unit/ICU) or **refer to burn centre** (face/hands/feet/genitalia/major joints, full-thickness, inhalation, electrical/chemical, > 10–20% TBSA, extremes of age/comorbidity)
- [ ] Titrate fluids to urine output; monitor for over-resuscitation (oedema/compartment/ARDS) and under-resuscitation
- [ ] Wound care/debridement; early nutrition; monitor for burn sepsis; DVT prophylaxis
- [ ] Chemical burn → copious irrigation; electrical burn → cardiac monitoring + rhabdomyolysis care ([[Rhabdomyolysis]])

> [!info]- Why
> Airway oedema progresses fast, so inhalation injury needs pre-emptive intubation; burns cause massive capillary leak needing structured Lactated-Ringer's resuscitation titrated to urine output (both under- and over-resuscitation harm). Circumferential eschar causes compartment syndrome. Verified vs ABA/burn-care guidance + Parkland formula.
