# Post-op Hysteroscopic Resection — Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local protocol and the attending. **Fluid-overload / hyponatraemia (TUR-like syndrome) is the key hazard** when large volumes of hypotonic distension media are used — check the intra-op fluid deficit; watch for confusion, nausea, visual change → send electrolytes urgently. Watch for uterine perforation, bleeding per vagina, and (rarely) gas embolism. **Send resected tissue for histopathology.**

## 🔴 One Day
- [ ] Confirm what was resected (endometrial polyp / submucous myoma / uterine septum / intrauterine adhesion — Asherman) — determines adjunct therapy + follow-up; note **distension media type + fluid deficit**
- [ ] Routine post-op care; observe bleeding per vagina; **check electrolytes (Na) if large fluid deficit or symptoms** (TUR-like syndrome)
- [ ] **Send specimen for histopathology**
- [ ] Transcervical procedure — no abdominal wound; once awake + tolerating oral → **off IV** (rapid recovery, usually same-day)

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Record vital signs + observe bleeding per vagina; resume regular diet
- [ ] **Paracetamol 500 mg PO q4–6h PRN** (#20) for cramping
- [ ] **Metronidazole 400 mg PO tid ×  ___ days** (#21) — instrumentation prophylaxis per protocol
- [ ] **After septum resection or adhesiolysis — cyclical estrogen ± progestin** (e.g. estradiol valerate/norgestrel pack ×  1 cycle) to regenerate endometrium and reduce re-adhesion; ± intrauterine barrier per surgeon
- [ ] Discharge advice: return for heavy bleeding/fever; follow-up OPD + histology; **repeat hysteroscopy/imaging to confirm cavity** after adhesiolysis/septum

> [!info]- Why
> Hysteroscopic resection is transcervical (fast recovery, no laparotomy) but carries a distinct fluid-absorption risk from the distension media (hyponatraemia/overload), so fluid balance and electrolytes are the safety focus; after septum/adhesion work, cyclical estrogen promotes endometrial regeneration and lowers re-adhesion rates. Verified vs AAGL hysteroscopy + fluid-management standards.
