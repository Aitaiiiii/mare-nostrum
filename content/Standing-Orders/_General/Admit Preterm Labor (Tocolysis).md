# Preterm Labor (Tocolysis) — Admission Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local protocol and the attending. **Tocolysis buys ~48 h for steroids + transfer — it is not long-term.** **Give antenatal corticosteroids** (the main fetal benefit) and **magnesium for neuroprotection < 32 wk**. **Do NOT tocolyse if: chorioamnionitis, severe pre-eclampsia/eclampsia, abruption, fetal demise, or non-reassuring fetal status.** Exclude PPROM.

## 🔴 One Day
- [ ] Confirm preterm labor (regular contractions + cervical change at **24–34 wk**); **exclude PPROM** ([[Admit PPROM]]) and contraindications to tocolysis
- [ ] CBC, UA; **cervical/vaginal swab (GBS) G/S + C/S**; EFM + monitor contractions
- [ ] **Antenatal corticosteroids: dexamethasone 6 mg IM q12h ×4 (or betamethasone 12 mg IM q24h ×2)** — fetal lung maturity (the priority)
- [ ] **Magnesium sulfate for fetal neuroprotection if < 32 wk + delivery expected < 24 h**
- [ ] **Tocolysis (≤ 48 h) — first-line nifedipine: 20 mg PO then 10–20 mg q4–6h** (keep BP > 90/60; recheck 15 min after doses) — OR **terbutaline** (β-agonist; keep maternal HR < 120, FHR < 160; watch pulmonary oedema — 2nd-line) — OR atosiban where available
- [ ] **GBS intrapartum prophylaxis** if delivery likely

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Admit — Dx: preterm labor at ___ wk
- [ ] Continuous EFM + contraction monitoring; **wean tocolysis when contractions settle** (do not continue maintenance tocolysis long-term — no benefit)
- [ ] If on terbutaline → monitor HR, fluid balance, glucose/electrolytes; watch for pulmonary oedema
- [ ] Neonatology involvement; **if labour progresses → allow delivery** (tocolysis fails); plan mode/place of delivery by GA
- [ ] Complete steroid course; treat any underlying cause (UTI/infection)

> [!info]- Why
> The fetal benefit comes from the 48-hour window for antenatal steroids (and magnesium neuroprotection + transfer to a unit with NICU), not from stopping labour indefinitely; nifedipine is first-line tocolytic with a good safety profile, terbutaline a second-line with more maternal risk. Tocolysis is withheld when continuing the pregnancy is dangerous (infection/abruption/severe pre-eclampsia/distress). Verified vs ACOG preterm-labor guidance.
