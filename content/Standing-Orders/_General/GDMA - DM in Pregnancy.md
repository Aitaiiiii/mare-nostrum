# GDMA / DM in Pregnancy

- **ชนิด** — GDMA1 (diet-controlled) vs GDMA2 (ต้องใช้ insulin) vs overt DM
- **เป้า DTX** — premeal < 95, 1 hr PP < 140, 2 hr PP < 120 mg%
- **Fetal surveillance + timing of delivery**
- **Postpartum 75 g OGTT ที่ 6 สัปดาห์**




## 🕐 One-Day Orders

> [!example]+ 🔬 Investigation
>
> - [ ] **DTX premeal tid + 1 hr PP tid + 2 hr PP tid** (สลับวันกัน)
> - [ ] **Record vital signs**
>
> ---
>

> [!example]+ 💊 Medication
>
> - [ ] **DM diet (C:P:F 40:20:40, total 2,000 kcal/day)**
> - [ ] **RI 6-6-6 U SC ac** (GDMA2)
> - [ ] **Triferdine 1×1 PO pc #60**
> - [ ] **CaCO3 1×1 PO pc #60**
>


## 🔁 Continuous Orders

> [!example]+ 🔬 Investigation
>
> - [ ] **DTX ต่อตามรอบ; ปรับ insulin ตามค่า**
>
