# Pelvic Inflammatory Disease / TOA — Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local STI guideline/antibiogram. **Treat empirically on a LOW threshold** (protect fertility). **β-hCG — exclude ectopic.** Metronidazole is now routine (CDC 2021). TOA → admit ± drain.

## 🔴 One Day
- [ ] **β-hCG** (exclude ectopic); NAAT gonorrhoea/chlamydia (± M. genitalium/trichomonas); **HIV + syphilis screen**; urinalysis, CBC/CRP
- [ ] **Transvaginal ultrasound** (TOA/tubal changes) if severe/mass/not improving
- [ ] **Empiric treatment on minimum criteria** (pelvic pain + cervical-motion/uterine/adnexal tenderness):
  - **Outpatient:** **Ceftriaxone 500 mg IM ×1 + Doxycycline 100 mg PO bid × 14 d + Metronidazole 500 mg PO bid × 14 d**
  - **Inpatient/parenteral (severe/TOA/pregnant/failed outpatient/can't tolerate oral):** **Ceftriaxone 1 g IV q24h + Doxycycline 100 mg IV/PO q12h + Metronidazole 500 mg IV/PO q12h**
- [ ] Analgesia; **treat sexual partners + advise abstinence**

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Admit if severe/TOA/pregnant/failed outpatient — Dx: PID (± TOA)
- [ ] **Reassess at 72 h**; transition IV → oral to complete 14 days
- [ ] **TOA → IV antibiotics + image-guided/surgical drainage** if large (> ~7 cm), no response 48–72 h, or ruptured (surgical emergency)
- [ ] **Remove IUD only if no clinical improvement** after antibiotics; STI counselling + repeat screening; contact tracing

> [!info]- Why
> PID sequelae (infertility, ectopic, chronic pain) rise with delay, so empiric treatment on minimal criteria is standard; the 2021 CDC regimen adds metronidazole for anaerobic cover. TOA needs anaerobic cover and often drainage. β-hCG excludes the dangerous ectopic mimic. Verified vs CDC 2021 STI guidelines.
