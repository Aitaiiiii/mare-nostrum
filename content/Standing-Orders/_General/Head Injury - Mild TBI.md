# Head Injury / Traumatic Brain Injury — ER Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local protocol and the attending. **Use a validated CT rule (Canadian CT Head)** to decide imaging. **Reverse anticoagulation + CT for any anticoagulated head injury.** **Severe TBI (GCS ≤ 8) → intubate + neurosurgery + treat raised ICP.** Treat as trauma (C-spine) and beware the deteriorating "talk-and-die" extradural.

## 🔴 One Day
- [ ] ABCs + trauma survey; **C-spine precautions**; **GCS + pupils + focal neuro**; capillary glucose; check mechanism, LOC, amnesia, anticoagulation/antiplatelet, alcohol
- [ ] **Non-contrast CT head — indications (Canadian CT Head Rule / NICE):** GCS < 15 at 2 h, suspected open/depressed/basal skull fracture, ≥ 2 vomits, age ≥ 65, dangerous mechanism, retrograde amnesia > 30 min, **any anticoagulant/coagulopathy**, post-traumatic seizure, focal deficit
- [ ] **Reverse anticoagulation** (4F-PCC/idarucizumab/andexanet, vitamin K) for intracranial bleed/high suspicion — see [[Intracerebral Hemorrhage (ICH)]]
- [ ] Analgesia (avoid over-sedation obscuring neuro exam); antiemetic; tetanus prophylaxis for wounds
- [ ] **Severe TBI (GCS ≤ 8): intubate (RSI), head up 30°, avoid hypoxia/hypotension (SBP targets), treat raised ICP (hypertonic saline/mannitol, brief hyperventilation for herniation), urgent neurosurgery**

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Disposition: **normal CT + low-risk + GCS 15 → observe/discharge with head-injury advice**; abnormal CT/persistent symptoms → admit ± neurosurgery
- [ ] Serial **neuro observations + GCS**; repeat CT for deterioration; treat seizures
- [ ] Severe TBI → neuro-ICU, ICP management, avoid secondary insults (hypoxia/hypotension/hyper- or hypocapnia/hyper-/hypoglycaemia/fever)
- [ ] Manage associated injuries; concussion/return-to-activity counselling for mild TBI

> [!info]- Why
> Most head injuries are mild, so validated CT rules select who needs imaging while avoiding unnecessary scans; anticoagulated patients and GCS deterioration are the danger groups (expanding extradural/subdural). Severe TBI outcome depends on preventing secondary brain injury (hypoxia/hypotension) and timely neurosurgery. Verified vs Canadian CT Head Rule / NICE + Brain Trauma Foundation.
