# MVA / Fractional Curettage / LEEP — Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local protocol and the attending. **Conscious sedation (pethidine + diazepam) depresses respiration — monitor RR + SpO₂ continuously during and after, have naloxone/flumazenil + airway ready.** **Give anti-D immunoglobulin if Rh-negative** (evacuation of pregnancy). Watch for excessive bleeding, uterine perforation, and retained products. **Send all specimens for histopathology.**

## 🔴 One Day
- [ ] Confirm indication: **MVA** (incomplete/missed abortion), **fractional curettage** (abnormal uterine bleeding — endocervical + endometrial sampling), or **LEEP** (CIN/cervical dysplasia); confirm GA + uterine size before MVA; **check Rh status**
- [ ] Admit gynae ward; **NPO; void before the procedure**; CBC + UA; **Hct STAT**
- [ ] **IV: Acetar (balanced crystalloid) 1000 mL IV at 120 mL/h**; set MVA/procedure tray at bedside
- [ ] **Conscious sedation before procedure: pethidine 50 mg IV + diazepam 10 mg IV** (titrate; monitor RR + SpO₂) — analgesia/sedation for a painful procedure
- [ ] **Anti-D immunoglobulin if Rh-negative**; misoprostol for cervical priming per protocol (LEEP/curettage as indicated)
- [ ] **Send specimen for histopathology** (products of conception / endometrial + endocervical / cervical cone)
- [ ] Once fully awake + tolerating oral → **off IV**; observe bleeding per vagina before discharge

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Record vital signs; **observe bleeding per vagina** (pad count) + cramping; resume regular diet
- [ ] **Paracetamol 500 mg PO q4–6h PRN** (#20) for cramping
- [ ] **Doxycycline 100 mg PO bid ×  ___ days** (#10) — post-procedure infection prophylaxis (uterine instrumentation)
- [ ] Discharge advice: return for heavy bleeding, fever, or foul discharge; **contraception counselling** (post-abortion) + histology follow-up; LEEP → colposcopy/cytology follow-up

> [!info]- Why
> These are short transcervical procedures whose main risks are pain (needing monitored sedation), haemorrhage/perforation, infection (justifying doxycycline prophylaxis), and — for pregnancy evacuation — Rh sensitization (anti-D). Histopathology is mandatory to exclude malignancy/molar disease and confirm complete evacuation. Verified vs WHO safe-abortion + gynaecology procedural standards.
