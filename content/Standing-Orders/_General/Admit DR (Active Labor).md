# Admit Delivery Room (Active Labor) — Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local protocol and the attending. **Confirm active labor** (regular painful contractions + cervix ≥ ~5–6 cm). **GBS-positive or ROM > 18 h + risk → intrapartum penicillin.** Continuous EFM for high-risk/oxytocin/epidural. Active third-stage management (uterotonic) prevents PPH. Watch for chorioamnionitis.

## 🔴 One Day (admission)
- [ ] Confirm labor + stage; **GA, presentation, EFM admission strip**; assess membranes (SROM/PROM — pooling/nitrazine/ferning)
- [ ] **CBC, blood group/Rh**, urine dipstick (protein/glucose); RBS if GDM/DM; GBS swab if status unknown
- [ ] **IV access (NSS lock or maintenance KVO)**; NPO to clear liquids in active phase
- [ ] **CTG/EFM** (admission 20 min → continuous if high-risk/oxytocin/epidural); **IE (dilatation/effacement/station/position) q4h** in active phase
- [ ] **[GBS positive / ROM > 18 h + risk] Penicillin G 5 MU IV then 2.5 MU IV q4h until delivery** (Cefazolin/clindamycin if penicillin allergy)
- [ ] **Oxytocin augmentation only for inadequate contractions (attending-directed):** oxytocin 5–10 IU in 500–1000 mL, start low (~1–2 mU/min), titrate q30 min with EFM
- [ ] Analgesia/epidural per request + anaesthesia

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Monitor labour progress (partogram); maternal vitals q30–60 min (temp q4h); continuous EFM if indicated
- [ ] **[Chorioamnionitis: fever + uterine tenderness/fetal tachycardia] ampicillin + gentamicin (± metronidazole) + expedite delivery**
- [ ] **Active management of the third stage: oxytocin 10 IU IM after delivery** (± controlled cord traction) — prevents PPH ([[Postpartum Hemorrhage (PPH)]])
- [ ] Prepare neonatal resuscitation; escalate for arrest of labour/fetal distress → operative/instrumental delivery or C-S
- [ ] Post-delivery → [[Post-Normal Labor (SVD)]] / post-C-S orders

> [!info]- Why
> Safe intrapartum care = confirm labour, monitor mother + fetus (partogram/EFM), give GBS prophylaxis when indicated, augment cautiously, and actively manage the third stage with a uterotonic to prevent postpartum haemorrhage; chorioamnionitis needs antibiotics + delivery.
