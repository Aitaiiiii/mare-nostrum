# PPROM (Preterm Premature Rupture of Membranes) — Admission Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local protocol and the attending. **Confirm ROM (sterile speculum: pooling/nitrazine/ferning) — avoid digital exams (infection).** Give **antenatal corticosteroids + latency antibiotics + (GA-dependent) magnesium for neuroprotection**. **Deliver immediately for chorioamnionitis, abruption, fetal distress, or labour** — expectant management otherwise (by GA).

## 🔴 One Day
- [ ] **Confirm ROM: sterile speculum** (pooling, nitrazine, ferning) + GA; **minimise digital examinations**
- [ ] CBC, CRP, UA; **cervical/vaginal swab (incl. GBS) G/S + C/S**; group & save
- [ ] **EFM + monitor for contractions/labour**; assess for chorioamnionitis
- [ ] **Antenatal corticosteroids (GA 24–34 wk, consider to 36+6): dexamethasone 6 mg IM q12h ×4 (or betamethasone 12 mg IM q24h ×2)** — fetal lung maturity
- [ ] **Latency antibiotics (to prolong pregnancy + reduce infection): ampicillin 2 g IV q6h ×48 h → amoxicillin PO, PLUS erythromycin** (course ~7 days) — avoid amoxicillin-clavulanate (NEC risk)
- [ ] **Magnesium sulfate for fetal neuroprotection if < 32 wk + imminent delivery**
- [ ] Tocolysis is generally NOT recommended in PPROM (only briefly to allow steroids/transfer if no infection — see [[Admit Preterm Labor (Tocolysis)]])

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Admit — Dx: PPROM at ___ wk; expectant management vs delivery by GA + status
- [ ] **Monitor for chorioamnionitis (fever ≥ 38 °C, uterine tenderness, maternal tachycardia > 120, fetal tachycardia > 160, foul liquor) → antibiotics + DELIVER**; serial CBC/CRP, temperature, fetal monitoring
- [ ] Complete steroid + antibiotic courses; **deliver at ~34 wk (or per local policy up to 37 wk) or earlier for infection/distress/labour/abruption**
- [ ] Neonatology involvement; document liquor/pad observation

> [!info]- Why
> PPROM management balances prematurity against ascending infection: steroids reduce neonatal morbidity, latency antibiotics prolong the pregnancy and reduce infection, and magnesium neuroprotects the very preterm fetus — while chorioamnionitis/distress/labour trigger immediate delivery. Digital exams are avoided to limit infection. Verified vs ACOG PPROM guidance.
