# Tubal Resection (TR) — Set OR + Post-op Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local protocol and the attending. **Confirm valid informed consent for permanent sterilization** (irreversible — counsel on permanence + failure rate ~ 1/200, and that a future pregnancy after failure is more likely ectopic). Note timing: **postpartum TR** (immediately after delivery) vs **interval TR**. Watch for bleeding, bowel/vascular injury, and (postpartum) uterine atony.

## 🔴 One Day (Set OR + immediate post-op)
- [ ] Confirm consent + completed family size; confirm timing (postpartum vs interval); **NPO after midnight; void before OR**
- [ ] Routine pre-op check (CBC/UA as indicated); **IV: Acetar 1000 mL IV at 120 mL/h**
- [ ] Post-op routine care; **observe bleeding per vagina + wound**; once awake + tolerating oral → **off IV**

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Record vital signs; **observe bleeding per vagina + wound**; resume regular diet
- [ ] **Paracetamol 500 mg PO q4–6h PRN** (#20) for pain
- [ ] Continue home medications; discharge advice: return for fever, heavy bleeding, or worsening abdominal pain; wound review + follow-up; **counsel that sterilization is effective immediately but does not protect against STIs**

> [!info]- Why
> Tubal resection is a short, low-morbidity procedure whose central issue is ethical/consent (permanence + the ectopic risk if it fails) rather than complex post-op management; recovery is rapid with minimal-IV, oral-analgesia orders, and the surgical vigilance points are bleeding and visceral injury. Verified vs ACOG sterilization + gynaecology procedural standards.
