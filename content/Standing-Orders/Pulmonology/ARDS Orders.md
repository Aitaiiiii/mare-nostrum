# ARDS — ICU Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local protocol and the attending. **Lung-protective ventilation (low tidal volume) is the intervention that saves lives.** **Treat the underlying cause** (sepsis/pneumonia/aspiration). Berlin: bilateral infiltrates + PaO₂/FiO₂ ≤ 300 (not fully explained by cardiac failure/overload), within 1 week of an insult.

## 🔴 One Day
- [ ] Confirm Berlin criteria + severity (**P/F: mild 200–300 · moderate 100–200 · severe ≤ 100** on PEEP ≥ 5); ABG, CXR/CT, echo (exclude cardiogenic oedema)
- [ ] **Identify + treat the cause** — sepsis/pneumonia (cultures + antibiotics — see [[Sepsis (Sepsis-3)]]), aspiration, pancreatitis, transfusion, etc.
- [ ] **Lung-protective ventilation:** **tidal volume 6 mL/kg predicted body weight**, **plateau pressure < 30 cmH₂O**, driving pressure < 15; PEEP per FiO₂/PEEP table; permissive hypercapnia (pH > 7.20)
- [ ] Target **SpO₂ 88–95% / PaO₂ 55–80**; **conservative fluid strategy** (net even/negative once perfusion adequate — FACTT)
- [ ] Analgesia/sedation (light where possible); assess for refractory hypoxaemia

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Admit ICU — Dx: ARDS (cause ___, severity ___)
- [ ] **Moderate-severe (P/F < 150) → prone positioning ≥ 16 h/day** (PROSEVA — mortality benefit); **neuromuscular blockade** for severe/ventilator dyssynchrony (short course)
- [ ] Continue lung-protective settings; daily sedation/spontaneous-breathing assessment; wean as improving
- [ ] **Refractory hypoxaemia → recruitment/ECMO referral** (VV-ECMO — see [[VV-ECMO]]); avoid fluid overload
- [ ] VTE + stress-ulcer prophylaxis; nutrition; treat the driver; DO NOT routinely give steroids unless indicated by cause (e.g. severe CAP/COVID per evidence)

> [!info]- Why
> ARDS mortality is reduced by low-tidal-volume ("baby lung") ventilation that limits ventilator-induced lung injury, prone positioning in moderate-severe disease (PROSEVA), and conservative fluids (FACTT) — while treating the precipitating cause. ECMO rescues refractory hypoxaemia. Verified vs ARDSNet / PROSEVA / ATS-ESICM ARDS.
