# Asthma Exacerbation — ER/Admission Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local protocol and the attending. **Silent chest, cyanosis, drowsiness, or rising/normal PaCO₂ = life-threatening → call ICU, prepare to intubate.** Treat wheeze + urticaria/hypotension/known trigger as [[Anaphylaxis]] (IM adrenaline) first.

## 🔴 One Day
- [ ] Assess severity NOW (talk/RR/pulse/SpO₂/PEF) while starting O₂ + SABA — do not delay treatment
- [ ] O₂ via nasal cannula/mask, titrate to **SpO₂ 93–95%**
- [ ] PEF (or FEV₁) before treatment and again at 1 hour; SpO₂ continuous monitoring
- [ ] **Salbutamol 2.5 mg NEB (O₂-driven) OR 4–10 puffs pMDI + spacer — repeat q20min ×3 in the first hour**
- [ ] **[SEVERE] Ipratropium bromide 500 mcg NEB (or 4 puffs 20 mcg pMDI) with salbutamol q20min ×3** (may combine as salbutamol+ipratropium neb)
- [ ] **Prednisolone 40–50 mg PO stat** (give within the first hour) — or **Hydrocortisone 100 mg IV** if vomiting/unable to swallow / too breathless
- [ ] **[SEVERE, not responding at 1 h or PEF < 60% predicted] Magnesium sulfate 2 g IV over 20 min** (mix 2 g in 100 mL NSS) — single dose
- [ ] ABG if SpO₂ < 90–92% on treatment, severe/life-threatening features, or deterioration (look for normal/high PaCO₂)
- [ ] CBC, electrolytes (watch **K⁺** — β2-agonist + steroid → hypokalaemia)
- [ ] CXR **only if** suspected pneumothorax/consolidation, diagnostic doubt, or no improvement — not routine
- [ ] Reassess at 1 hour → good response (PEF ≥ 60% predicted, stable, SpO₂ ≥ 94% air) = plan discharge · poor response = admit · life-threatening = ICU + intubation

> [!info]- Why
> Repeated SABA + **early systemic steroid** is the outcome-changing bundle; steroid works within ~4 h so give in the first hour. Ipratropium added to SABA reduces admissions in severe attacks. A single 2 g IV magnesium cuts admissions in severe non-responders (nebulised magnesium is **no longer recommended**, GINA 2025). Controlled O₂ to 93–95% avoids worsening hypercapnia. Antibiotics/CXR are not routine (most attacks are viral). Verified vs GINA 2025 + TAC 2025.

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Admit to Medicine (or ICU if life-threatening) — Dx: Acute asthma exacerbation, severity ___
- [ ] Condition; vital signs + SpO₂ q1–4h per severity; strict I/O
- [ ] **Salbutamol NEB q1–4h and PRN**, titrate frequency to response (± ipratropium q6h while severe)
- [ ] **Prednisolone 40–50 mg PO once daily to complete 5–7 days total** (no taper for short course) — or IV hydrocortisone until able to swallow
- [ ] **Start/continue ICS-containing controller BEFORE discharge** — ICS–formoterol (MART) or medium-dose ICS-LABA; **do not discharge on SABA alone**
- [ ] KCl replacement per K⁺; regular diet as tolerated
- [ ] Discharge bundle: inhaler-technique check, written asthma action plan, identify trigger, **follow-up 2–7 days**

> [!info]- Why
> The controller switch (ICS-containing, ideally MART) is what prevents the next attack and reduces asthma death; SABA-only discharge is unsafe. Short OCS courses need no taper. Ongoing nebs are stepped down to PRN as the patient improves. K⁺ falls with intensive β2-agonist + steroid.
