# Pneumothorax — ER/Admission Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local protocol and the attending. **Tension pneumothorax = clinical diagnosis → decompress immediately, do NOT wait for CXR.** Never clamp a bubbling drain. Treat SSP more aggressively than PSP.

## 🔴 One Day
- [ ] **[TENSION — hypotension + absent breath sounds + hyperresonance] Immediate needle decompression:** large-bore cannula, **2nd ICS mid-clavicular line** OR **4th/5th ICS anterior-mid axillary line**, then definitive chest drain
- [ ] High-flow O₂ to keep SpO₂ ≥ 94% (helps pleural air reabsorption)
- [ ] Erect **CXR** (confirm + describe); **CT chest** if small/loculated, trauma, bullae vs pneumothorax, or surgical planning; POCUS in resus/supine trauma
- [ ] CBC, coagulation profile (pre-procedure), group & save if trauma/haemopneumothorax
- [ ] **Decide by symptoms + high-risk characteristics, NOT size:**
  - High-risk (any: haemodynamic compromise, significant hypoxia, bilateral, underlying lung disease, **≥ 50 + significant smoking**, haemopneumothorax) → **intercostal chest drain (small-bore Seldinger ≤ 14 F)**
  - Minimally symptomatic / asymptomatic, no high-risk → **conservative observation regardless of size**
  - Symptomatic, low-risk → **needle aspiration (14–16 G, ≤ 2.5 L)** OR **ambulatory one-way-valve device** OR small-bore drain
- [ ] Chest drain into the **safe triangle** (5th ICS, lat. border pec major, ant. border lat dorsi), above the rib; underwater seal; **do not clamp if bubbling**
- [ ] Analgesia: paracetamol ± opioid; local anaesthetic for procedure
- [ ] Post-procedure CXR to confirm position + re-expansion

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Admit (ward; monitored/ICU if tension, SSP, or unstable) — Dx: pneumothorax (type ___, side ___)
- [ ] Vital signs + SpO₂ q1–4h; monitor drain: swing, bubbling (air leak), drainage volume
- [ ] Daily CXR while drain in situ; document air leak trend
- [ ] **Suction NOT routine** — add only for non-resolution, senior-led, high-volume low-pressure
- [ ] Remove drain when lung re-expanded + no air leak (± clamping trial per local protocol), then CXR
- [ ] **Persistent air leak > 3–5 days or failure to re-expand → thoracic surgery (VATS) referral**
- [ ] Recurrence prevention referral: 2nd ipsilateral / 1st contralateral or bilateral / high-risk occupation → **VATS + pleurodesis**
- [ ] Counsel: **smoking cessation**; **no flying until radiographic resolution**; **no scuba diving ever** (unless definitive bilateral surgery)

> [!info]- Why
> Tension physiology kills by obstructive shock — decompression precedes imaging. BTS 2023 treats by symptoms and risk, sparing stable patients an unnecessary drain; small-bore Seldinger drains and ambulatory devices shorten stay with equal recurrence. Clamping a bubbling drain can re-create tension. Diving carries a high fatal-recurrence risk. Verified vs BTS 2023.
