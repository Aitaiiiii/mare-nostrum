# Thyroid Storm — Admission Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local protocol and the attending. Clinical diagnosis (**Burch-Wartofsky score**) — treat on suspicion, don't wait for TFTs. **Give the thionamide (PTU) BEFORE iodine** (iodine before blockade can fuel hormone synthesis). Treat the precipitant. Multi-pronged: block synthesis, block release, block peripheral effects, block conversion, support.

## 🔴 One Day (the 5 targets)
- [ ] **1. Block synthesis — thionamide: Propylthiouracil (PTU) 500–1000 mg PO/NG loading, then 250 mg q4h** (preferred in storm — also blocks T4→T3 conversion); or methimazole 20–25 mg q4–6h
- [ ] **2. Block release — iodine ≥ 1 h AFTER the thionamide: Lugol's solution / SSKI** (or sodium iodide) — never before the thionamide
- [ ] **3. Block adrenergic effects — Propranolol 60–80 mg PO q4–6h (or 0.5–1 mg IV slow, titrated)** — controls HR, tremor, agitation (also blocks T4→T3); caution/avoid in decompensated HF (use short-acting esmolol + monitor)
- [ ] **4. Block conversion + treat relative adrenal insufficiency — Hydrocortisone 100 mg IV q8h** (or dexamethasone)
- [ ] **5. Supportive — active cooling + paracetamol (NOT aspirin — displaces T4)**, IV fluids/dextrose, treat agitation
- [ ] **Treat the precipitant** (infection/sepsis, DKA, MI, surgery, iodine load, non-adherence) — septic screen
- [ ] Labs: TFT (free T4/T3, TSH), CBC, electrolytes, glucose, LFT, calcium, cultures; ECG (AF/tachyarrhythmia); continuous monitoring

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Admit **ICU** — Dx: thyroid storm (Burch-Wartofsky ___, precipitant ___)
- [ ] Continue thionamide + iodine + β-blocker + steroid until improving; taper steroid/iodine as storm resolves
- [ ] Manage complications: high-output/decompensated HF, AF (rate control/anticoagulation), hyperthermia, dehydration
- [ ] Monitor TFT trend, LFT (PTU hepatotoxicity/agranulocytosis — check CBC if fever/sore throat)
- [ ] Definitive therapy planning after recovery (radioactive iodine/surgery); endocrine follow-up

> [!info]- Why
> Storm is decompensated thyrotoxicosis with high mortality, so treatment is empiric and multi-pronged: the thionamide blocks new hormone synthesis and must precede iodine (which otherwise supplies substrate — Jod-Basedow); β-blockers and steroids blunt peripheral effects and T4→T3 conversion; aspirin is avoided (frees T4). Treating the precipitant is essential. Verified vs Endocrine Society Thailand 2568 + ATA.
