# Myxedema Coma — ICU/Admission Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local endocrine protocol and the attending. **Give hydrocortisone BEFORE/with thyroid hormone** (adrenal-crisis prevention). **Passive rewarming only.** Support ventilation (CO₂ narcosis); **avoid sedatives/opioids.** Always treat the precipitant (infection — fever may be absent).

## 🔴 One Day
- [ ] **Draw random cortisol + TFTs** (start treatment on suspicion, don't wait); glucose, **Na⁺**, ABG/VBG (CO₂), CBC, renal/liver, CK, ECG
- [ ] **Hydrocortisone 100 mg IV STAT, then 50 mg IV q6–8h** — before/with thyroid hormone
- [ ] **Levothyroxine (T4) ~4 µg/kg (200–400 µg) IV loading, then 50–100 µg IV daily** (± **liothyronine T3** in the sickest, cautiously in cardiac disease)
- [ ] **Passive rewarming** (blankets); **avoid aggressive active external rewarming**
- [ ] **Ventilatory support** for hypoventilation/CO₂ narcosis (low threshold to intubate)
- [ ] **Treat hypoglycaemia** (IV dextrose); **hyponatraemia** (fluid restrict ± cautious hypertonic saline if severe/symptomatic — no over-rapid correction)
- [ ] Cautious fluids/pressors for hypotension; **empiric antibiotics + septic screen** (precipitant)
- [ ] **Avoid sedatives/opioids/anaesthetics** (impaired metabolism)

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Admit **ICU** — Dx: myxedema coma (precipitant ___)
- [ ] Continuous temp, cardiac monitor, SpO₂, GCS; strict I/O
- [ ] Continue IV levothyroxine (± T3) → transition to oral once improving; continue hydrocortisone until adrenal insufficiency excluded, then taper
- [ ] Serial Na⁺, glucose, gases; monitor for arrhythmia/ischaemia (esp. with T3)
- [ ] Identify + fully treat precipitant (infection, MI, stroke, GI bleed, cold, drugs)
- [ ] Endocrine follow-up; establish maintenance levothyroxine + patient education

> [!info]- Why
> Severe hypothyroidism slows every organ — the deaths are from CO₂ narcosis, hypothermia-related collapse, and untreated infection/adrenal crisis. Thyroid hormone alone can precipitate adrenal crisis, so steroid comes first; active rewarming causes vasodilatory shock; sedatives accumulate. Verified vs endocrine guidance.
