# Adrenal Crisis — ER Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local protocol and the attending. **Give hydrocortisone on suspicion — do NOT wait for cortisol.** Suspect in shock refractory to fluids/pressors with hyponatraemia/hyperkalaemia/hypoglycaemia. Stress-dose any steroid-dependent patient who is ill.

## 🔴 One Day
- [ ] **Hydrocortisone 100 mg IV (or IM) STAT**, then **200 mg/24 h continuous IV infusion OR 50 mg IV/IM q6h**
- [ ] (If seconds allow) draw **random serum cortisol ± ACTH/aldosterone/renin BEFORE hydrocortisone** — else give dexamethasone 4 mg (preserves later Synacthen test) and treat
- [ ] **Rapid isotonic saline ~1000 mL in the first hour**, then 4–6 L/24 h titrated to haemodynamics (watch overload in elderly/renal)
- [ ] **Treat hypoglycaemia** — IV dextrose (D50) then infusion
- [ ] Labs: electrolytes (Na⁺/K⁺), glucose, BUN/Cr, CBC, **septic screen** (find precipitant)
- [ ] Continuous monitor; identify + treat precipitant (infection, surgery, missed steroid, MI, adrenal haemorrhage)
- [ ] Hyperkalaemia/hyponatraemia usually correct with hydrocortisone + saline (see [[Dyskalemia (Hypo- & Hyperkalemia)]])

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Admit (ICU if shock) — Dx: adrenal crisis (?primary/?secondary, precipitant ___)
- [ ] Continue hydrocortisone 200 mg/24 h until stable, then **taper to oral maintenance**
- [ ] Monitor Na⁺ — **avoid over-rapid correction** as cortisol replacement induces water diuresis (osmotic demyelination risk)
- [ ] **Add fludrocortisone in primary AI** once hydrocortisone < 50 mg/day
- [ ] Complete workup: short Synacthen (ACTH-stimulation) test when stable; investigate cause (autoimmune/TB/pituitary)
- [ ] **Discharge: sick-day rules, emergency hydrocortisone IM kit, medical-alert bracelet, endocrine follow-up**

> [!info]- Why
> Cortisol maintains vascular tone and glucose, so its deficiency causes pressor-refractory shock that reverses only with steroid — given empirically because delay kills and the drug is safe. Stress-dose hydrocortisone covers mineralocorticoid needs acutely. Finding the precipitant (usually infection) and educating on sick-day rules prevent recurrence. Verified vs Endocrine Society / Society for Endocrinology.
