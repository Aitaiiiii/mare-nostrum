# Electrolyte Correction — Reference Orders

> [!danger] ⚠️ SAFETY
> Reference only; adjust to local protocol and the attending. **Hyperkalaemia with ECG changes = immediate.** **Correct sodium slowly** (≤ 8–10 mmol/L per 24 h — osmotic demyelination). Replace **magnesium** to correct refractory hypokalaemia/hypocalcaemia. Peripheral KCl infusion rate is limited (irritation/arrhythmia). Always find + treat the cause.

## 🔵 Potassium
- [ ] **Hyperkalaemia (ECG changes / K⁺ > 6.5):** **calcium gluconate 10% 10 mL IV** (membrane stabilisation, repeat) → **insulin 10 U + 25 g dextrose IV + salbutamol neb** (shift) → **removal** (loop diuretic, K-binder [patiromer/SZC/resonium], dialysis if refractory/AKI); recheck K⁺ + glucose
- [ ] **Hypokalaemia:** oral KCl preferred if able; IV KCl **≤ 10 mmol/h peripheral (≤ 20 mmol/h central with monitoring)**; **replace magnesium** (hypomagnesaemia causes refractory hypokalaemia); target > 4.0 in cardiac/arrhythmia patients

## 🔵 Sodium
- [ ] **Hyponatraemia:** assess volume + acuity/symptoms. **Severe symptomatic (seizure/coma): 3% hypertonic saline 100–150 mL IV bolus, repeat to Δ ~4–6 mmol/L**; **limit correction ≤ 8–10 mmol/L per 24 h** (osmotic demyelination); treat by type (hypovolaemic → NS; SIADH → fluid restrict; hypervolaemic → restrict + diuretic) — see [[Hyponatremia]]
- [ ] **Hypernatraemia:** correct water deficit slowly (≤ 10–12 mmol/L per 24 h) with oral water / hypotonic fluid; treat cause

## 🔵 Calcium / Magnesium / Phosphate
- [ ] **Hypocalcaemia (symptomatic/severe): calcium gluconate 10% IV** (bolus then infusion); correct **magnesium** and vitamin D; check albumin/ionised Ca — see [[Hypercalcemia & Hypocalcemia]]
- [ ] **Hypercalcaemia (severe/symptomatic): IV isotonic saline** + **calcitonin** (fast) + **IV bisphosphonate (zoledronate)** (sustained); treat cause (malignancy/hyperparathyroid)
- [ ] **Hypomagnesaemia: magnesium sulfate IV** (e.g. 1–2 g); needed to correct refractory K⁺/Ca²⁺
- [ ] **Hypophosphataemia (severe < 1 mg/dL / symptomatic): IV phosphate**; otherwise oral — see [[Hypophosphatemia]] (watch refeeding syndrome)

## 🔵 Continuous
- [ ] Recheck levels after correction; continuous ECG for severe K⁺/Ca²⁺ derangement; monitor for over-correction (Na⁺ especially)
- [ ] Identify + treat the underlying cause (drugs, renal, GI losses, endocrine)

> [!info]- Why
> Electrolyte emergencies kill via the heart (K⁺, Ca²⁺, Mg²⁺) or brain (Na⁺), so severe/symptomatic derangements are corrected promptly but sodium is corrected slowly to avoid osmotic demyelination; magnesium must be replaced to fix refractory hypokalaemia/hypocalcaemia.
