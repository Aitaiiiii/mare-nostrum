# HSV Encephalitis (Viral Encephalitis)

Inflammation of the brain parenchyma, most importantly the **treatable and devastating herpes simplex virus (HSV-1) encephalitis.** Untreated it has ~70% mortality and severe neurological sequelae, but **early aciclovir dramatically improves outcome** — so the rule is to **start empiric aciclovir immediately in anyone with fever + altered mentation, before the diagnosis is confirmed.**

## 🧬 Pathophysiology & causes

HSV-1 reaches the brain (reactivation or primary) with a predilection for the **medial temporal and inferior frontal lobes** → haemorrhagic necrotising encephalitis. Other causes: other herpesviruses (VZV, CMV, EBV, HHV-6), enteroviruses, arboviruses (**Japanese encephalitis — important in Thailand/rural Asia**, dengue, Nipah), rabies, and autoimmune encephalitis (anti-NMDA-receptor). Encephalitis (parenchymal → altered cognition/behaviour/focal signs) is distinguished from meningitis (meningeal → neck stiffness, preserved cognition), though overlap ("meningoencephalitis") is common.

## 🩺 Presentation

**Fever + altered mental status / behavioural or personality change / confusion**, with **headache, seizures (often focal, temporal), focal neurological deficits (dysphasia, hemiparesis), and psychiatric features** (temporal-lobe involvement → memory/behaviour/olfactory hallucinations). Progresses over hours–days to coma. A subacute or psychiatric-seeming presentation should not delay treatment.

## 🩻 Investigations (start aciclovir in parallel — do not delay)

- **Lumbar puncture (after CT if indicated):** CSF **lymphocytic pleocytosis, raised protein, normal/slightly low glucose, often RBCs/xanthochromia (haemorrhagic)**; **HSV PCR (sensitivity ~96%, specificity ~99%)** — **BUT a negative PCR in the first 24–72 h does NOT exclude HSV; continue aciclovir and repeat the PCR in 3–7 days** if suspicion remains.
- **MRI brain** — **temporal-lobe (± inferior frontal) T2/FLAIR hyperintensity ± restricted diffusion/haemorrhage** is characteristic (more sensitive than CT). **EEG** — temporal focal slowing/PLEDs.
- Bloods, cultures, **HIV test**; consider JE/arbovirus and autoimmune (anti-NMDAR) work-up per context.

## 💊 Management

**1. Empiric IV aciclovir — immediately.** **Aciclovir 10 mg/kg IV every 8 hours** started **as soon as encephalitis is suspected** (do not wait for LP/PCR/MRI). Ensure **good hydration** (aciclovir crystal nephropathy → give with IV fluids; dose-adjust in renal impairment). **Continue for 14–21 days** for confirmed HSV (≥ 21 days in the immunocompromised); **stop only if HSV is excluded** (negative PCR with a low-suspicion picture, or an alternative diagnosis).

**2. Cover the mimics empirically while working up.** If bacterial meningitis is also possible, **add empiric antibiotics ± dexamethasone** (see [[Bacterial Meningitis]]) until CSF/cultures clarify. Consider VZV (aciclovir covers it), and JE/arbovirus (supportive) in the Thai context.

**3. Supportive + complications.** **Treat seizures** (they are common), manage raised ICP/cerebral oedema, correct hyponatraemia (SIADH), and provide neuro-ICU support for depressed consciousness. Consider **autoimmune encephalitis** (anti-NMDAR — which can follow HSV encephalitis) if not improving/relapsing → immunotherapy.

**4. Follow-up.** Neurorehabilitation for sequelae (memory/behaviour/epilepsy); a repeat LP to confirm PCR clearance is sometimes used before stopping in complex cases.

## 🇹🇭 Thailand availability

IV aciclovir, CSF HSV PCR, MRI, and EEG are available at Thai hospitals (PCR turnaround may require send-out — treat empirically meanwhile). **Japanese encephalitis is endemic in Thailand** (rural, rice-paddy/pig exposure; vaccine-preventable and part of the Thai EPI) and is a key differential; rabies (animal exposure) and dengue encephalopathy are others. Empiric aciclovir + JE/arbovirus consideration is the Thai approach.

> [!danger] 🚨 Red flags / strict avoidance
> - **Start empiric IV aciclovir 10 mg/kg q8h IMMEDIATELY** in fever + altered mentation — before LP/PCR/MRI.
> - **A negative early CSF HSV PCR does NOT exclude it** — continue aciclovir and repeat PCR in 3–7 days.
> - **Hydrate + renally dose aciclovir** (crystal nephropathy).
> - **Cover bacterial meningitis empirically** if uncertain; consider **JE/arbovirus** in Thailand.
> - Not improving/relapsing → consider **autoimmune (anti-NMDAR) encephalitis** (can follow HSV).
