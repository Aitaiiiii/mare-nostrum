# Approach — Altered Mental Status & Coma

Altered mental status (from confusion/delirium to coma) is a final common pathway of countless diseases. The approach is **simultaneous resuscitation + reversal of immediate threats, a systematic differential, a focused neurological exam that localises, and targeted investigation** — because the causes span structural brain lesions, metabolic/toxic derangements, and infection, and several are instantly reversible.

## 💊 First: ABCs + immediate reversibles (do these before the work-up)

- **Airway/Breathing/Circulation** — protect the airway (**GCS ≤ 8 → intubate**), oxygenate, support circulation.
- **Capillary glucose → treat hypoglycaemia** (IV dextrose) immediately; **thiamine before/with glucose** in the malnourished/alcoholic (Wernicke).
- **Naloxone** if opioid toxidrome (↓RR + miosis) — see [[Opioid Overdose]].
- **Oxygen**; consider **empiric treatment** of the treatable time-critical causes if suspected (aciclovir + antibiotics for [[HSV Encephalitis|encephalitis]]/[[Bacterial Meningitis|meningitis]]; see below).

## 🧬 The differential — "AEIOU-TIPS"

| Letter | Cause |
|---|---|
| **A** | Alcohol / toxins ([[Common Toxidromes]], [[Toxic Alcohol Poisoning]]) |
| **E** | Endocrine ([[Myxedema Coma]], [[Adrenal Crisis]], [[Thyroid Storm]]), Electrolytes ([[Hyponatremia]], Ca, [[Dyskalemia (Hypo- & Hyperkalemia)\|K]]), Encephalopathy ([[Hepatic Encephalopathy\|hepatic]], uraemic, hypertensive) |
| **I** | Insulin — **hypo/hyperglycaemia** ([[DKA (Diabetic Ketoacidosis)\|DKA]], [[HHS (Hyperosmolar Hyperglycemic State)\|HHS]]) |
| **O** | Oxygen (hypoxia/[[Carbon Monoxide Poisoning\|CO]]), Opioids/overdose, CO₂ narcosis |
| **U** | Uraemia |
| **T** | Trauma (head injury, [[Intracerebral Hemorrhage (ICH)\|ICH]], [[Subarachnoid Hemorrhage (SAH)\|SAH]]), Temperature ([[Heat Stroke]], [[Accidental Hypothermia\|hypothermia]]) |
| **I** | Infection ([[Bacterial Meningitis\|meningitis]]/[[HSV Encephalitis\|encephalitis]], [[Sepsis (Sepsis-3)\|sepsis]]) |
| **P** | Psychiatric, Porphyria, Poisoning |
| **S** | [[Status Epilepticus\|Seizure/post-ictal/non-convulsive status]], Stroke, Shock, Space-occupying lesion, SAH |

## 🩺 Focused examination (localise structural vs diffuse)

**GCS/AVPU**, **pupils** (pinpoint → opioids/pontine; fixed dilated → herniation/anoxia; unequal → structural/uncal), **brainstem reflexes** (corneal, gag, oculocephalic), **respiratory pattern**, **fundoscopy** (papilloedema, subhyaloid haemorrhage), meningism, **focal neurological signs/asymmetry** (structural lesion → often lateralising) vs symmetrical (metabolic/toxic → usually non-focal), and general exam (temperature, skin, needle marks, breath — fetor hepaticus/ketones, injury). **Asterixis/myoclonus** suggest metabolic causes.

## 🩻 Investigations

Immediate: **glucose, electrolytes/Ca, renal + liver function, ammonia (if suspected), venous/arterial gas + CO-Hb, CBC, coagulation, toxicology + paracetamol/salicylate, TFT/cortisol as indicated, cultures**; ECG. **Urgent CT head** for focal signs, trauma, anticoagulation, headache/↑ICP concern, or no clear metabolic cause; **LP** (after CT if indicated) when **CNS infection/SAH** is suspected — and **do not delay empiric aciclovir + antibiotics** for the tap. **EEG** for suspected non-convulsive status epilepticus (a "not-waking" patient after a seizure or unexplained coma).

## 🇹🇭 Thailand availability

Bedside glucose, naloxone, thiamine, CT/LP, and EEG are available; consider Thai-relevant causes — **CNS/TB/JE/cryptococcal infection, dengue encephalopathy, cerebral malaria, organophosphate, methanol, and heat stroke**. The reversibles (glucose, thiamine, naloxone) plus a CT-and-LP pathway for infective/structural causes are the core.

> [!danger] 🚨 Red flags / strict avoidance
> - **Give glucose (with thiamine first if at risk) immediately**; **naloxone** for an opioid picture — reversibles before the work-up.
> - **GCS ≤ 8 → protect the airway** (intubate).
> - **Focal/lateralising signs or ↑ICP → urgent CT** before LP; **fever + AMS → empiric antibiotics + aciclovir now** (don't wait for the tap).
> - **Not waking after a seizure = non-convulsive status → EEG**; don't call it "post-ictal" indefinitely.
> - Always check a **paracetamol/salicylate/CO** level in the poisoned/unexplained patient; think adrenal/thyroid/hepatic/uraemic.

> [!tip] 🎯 High-Yield Recall
> - **ABCs + DEFG ("Don't Ever Forget Glucose")** + thiamine + naloxone first.
> - Differential = **AEIOU-TIPS**; exam separates **structural (focal/asymmetric, abnormal pupils/brainstem)** from **metabolic/toxic (symmetric, asterixis)**.
> - **CT for focal/trauma/↑ICP**; **LP for infection/SAH** (empiric aciclovir + antibiotics first); **EEG for non-convulsive status**.
> - Screen glucose, Na, Ca, renal/liver, gas + CO, tox (paracetamol/salicylate), TFT/cortisol.
> - Reversible causes are common and time-critical — don't anchor on "just intoxicated".
