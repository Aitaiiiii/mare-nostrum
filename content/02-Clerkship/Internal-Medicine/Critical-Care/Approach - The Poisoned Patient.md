# Approach — The Poisoned Patient

Poisoning (usually deliberate self-harm, sometimes accidental/occupational) is a common ER presentation where **most patients do well with good supportive care**, and a minority need a specific antidote or extracorporeal removal. This hub gives the generic approach — **resuscitate, recognise the toxidrome, screen the universal co-ingestants, decontaminate/eliminate where it helps, antidote when there is one, and get poison-centre advice** — routing to the specific agent pages.

## 💊 1. Resuscitate + universal measures

- **ABCs**; airway protection for the obtunded; oxygen; IV access, monitoring, ECG.
- **The "coma cocktail" reversibles:** **glucose** (check + treat hypoglycaemia), **thiamine** (before glucose in the malnourished), **naloxone** (opioid picture), and **oxygen**.
- **Continuous cardiac monitoring + 12-lead ECG** — look for **QRS widening** (sodium-channel blockers/[[TCA Overdose|TCA]] → bicarbonate) and **QT prolongation** (torsades risk).

## 🩺 2. Identify the toxidrome (pattern recognition)

| Toxidrome | Features | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Cholinergic** | SLUDGE/DUMBELS, bradycardia, miosis, secretions | [[Organophosphate Poisoning\|organophosphates]]/carbamates |
| **Anticholinergic** | dry, flushed, hyperthermic, mydriasis, delirium, tachycardia, retention | antihistamines, [[TCA Overdose\|TCAs]], atropine, datura |
| **Sympathomimetic** | agitation, mydriasis, tachycardia, hypertension, hyperthermia, **sweating** | cocaine, amphetamines/methamphetamine |
| **Opioid** | ↓consciousness, ↓RR, **miosis** | heroin, morphine, tramadol, methadone → [[Opioid Overdose]] |
| **Sedative-hypnotic** | ↓consciousness, normal pupils/vitals | benzodiazepines, alcohol, barbiturates |
| **Serotonergic** | clonus/hyperreflexia, agitation, hyperthermia | SSRIs, tramadol → [[Serotonin Syndrome & NMS]] |

Distinguish anticholinergic (dry, tachy, mydriatic) from cholinergic (wet, brady, miotic) — the mirror images most tested at the bedside. (Full detail: [[Common Toxidromes]].)

## 🩻 3. Investigations — screen the universals

**Always: capillary glucose, paracetamol level, salicylate level, ECG, and a venous/arterial gas + electrolytes (anion & osmolar gaps).** Add: renal/liver function, CK, lactate, coagulation, **carboxyhaemoglobin** (fires/heaters), pregnancy test, and **specific drug levels** where they change management (paracetamol, salicylate, lithium, digoxin, valproate, iron, theophylline, toxic alcohols). A **raised anion-gap metabolic acidosis** ± **osmolar gap** flags [[Toxic Alcohol Poisoning|toxic alcohols]]/[[Salicylate Overdose|salicylate]]/metformin.

## 4. Decontamination & enhanced elimination (selective, not routine)

- **Activated charcoal** — within ~1 h of a significant ingestion, airway protected; **not for** caustics, hydrocarbons, metals/lithium/iron, or the obtunded unprotected airway.
- **Whole-bowel irrigation** — sustained-release/enteric-coated drugs, iron, body packers.
- **Do NOT** induce emesis; gastric lavage almost never.
- **Enhanced elimination:** **urinary alkalinisation** (salicylate, phenobarbital); **haemodialysis** (the "I-STUMBLED"/toxic-alcohols, lithium, salicylate, valproate, metformin/lactic acidosis, theophylline); **multi-dose charcoal** (theophylline, carbamazepine, dapsone, phenobarbital); **lipid emulsion** for lipophilic cardiotoxins (see [[TCA Overdose]], [[Beta-Blocker & CCB Overdose]]).

## 5. Specific antidotes (know the common ones)

Paracetamol → **NAC** · opioids → **naloxone** · benzodiazepines → flumazenil (rarely — seizure risk) · [[Organophosphate Poisoning|organophosphates]] → **atropine + pralidoxime** · [[TCA Overdose|TCA]]/Na-channel → **sodium bicarbonate** · [[Beta-Blocker & CCB Overdose|β-blocker/CCB]] → **glucagon/calcium/high-dose insulin** · [[Toxic Alcohol Poisoning|methanol/ethylene glycol]] → **fomepizole** · [[Carbon Monoxide Poisoning|CO]] → **100% O₂/HBO** · iron → desferrioxamine · digoxin → **digoxin-specific Fab** · [[Serotonin Syndrome & NMS|serotonin syndrome]] → cyproheptadine · warfarin → vitamin K/PCC · sulfonylurea → octreotide/dextrose.

## 6. Disposition + psychosocial

Observe by agent (short for uncomplicated; prolonged/ICU for sustained-release, long-acting, or delayed-toxicity agents — e.g. paracetamol, sulfonylureas, toxic alcohols, calcium-channel blockers). **Every deliberate overdose needs a psychiatric/risk assessment before discharge.** **Call the poison centre — Ramathibodi Poison Center 1367** — for identification, dosing, and management advice.

## 🇹🇭 Thailand availability

Common antidotes (NAC, naloxone, atropine, pralidoxime, sodium bicarbonate, calcium, glucagon, digoxin-Fab where stocked, vitamin K) are available; fomepizole and some antidotes may be limited (ethanol drip as an alternative). Thai-relevant exposures: **paracetamol, tramadol, organophosphates/paraquat (agricultural), methanol (illicit liquor), kratom/datura/mushrooms, and snakebite** (separate pages). The **Ramathibodi Poison Center (1367)** is the 24-h resource.

> [!danger] 🚨 Red flags / strict avoidance
> - **Check glucose + paracetamol + salicylate + ECG in EVERY poisoned/overdose patient** (occult, treatable, and time-critical).
> - **Universal reversibles first** (glucose/thiamine/naloxone/O₂); airway for the obtunded.
> - **Decontamination/elimination are selective, not routine** — no ipecac; charcoal only if appropriate + airway protected.
> - **Sustained-release/long-acting/delayed-toxicity agents** need prolonged observation — don't discharge early.
> - **Psychiatric assessment for every deliberate overdose**; call **poison centre 1367**.

> [!tip] 🎯 High-Yield Recall
> - Resuscitate + **coma cocktail (glucose/thiamine/naloxone/O₂)** + ECG; treat the patient, not just the poison.
> - **Recognise the toxidrome** (cholinergic vs anticholinergic vs sympathomimetic vs opioid vs sedative vs serotonergic).
> - **Always screen paracetamol/salicylate/glucose/gas (anion + osmolar gap)**.
> - Selective **charcoal / WBI / alkalinisation / dialysis / lipid emulsion**; give the **specific antidote**.
> - Observe per agent; **psych review + poison centre 1367** for every deliberate overdose.
