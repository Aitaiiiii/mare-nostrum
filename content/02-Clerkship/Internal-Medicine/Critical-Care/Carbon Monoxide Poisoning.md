# Carbon Monoxide Poisoning

A common, easily-missed poisoning from incomplete combustion (house fires, charcoal/gas heaters and stoves in enclosed spaces, generators, car exhaust). The trap is that **pulse oximetry reads falsely normal**, so the diagnosis rests on suspicion and a **carboxyhaemoglobin (COHb) level**; the treatment is **high-flow oxygen**, which accelerates CO elimination.

## 🧬 Mechanism

CO binds haemoglobin with ~240× the affinity of oxygen → **carboxyhaemoglobin**, which (1) reduces oxygen-carrying capacity, (2) **left-shifts the oxygen–haemoglobin dissociation curve** (impairing tissue O₂ release), and (3) binds cytochrome oxidase (impairing cellular respiration) → **tissue hypoxia** despite a normal PaO₂. CO also triggers lipid peroxidation and inflammation → **delayed neurological sequelae.** High-flow oxygen shortens the CO half-life (air ~4–5 h → 100% O₂ ~60–90 min → hyperbaric ~20–30 min).

## 🩺 Presentation

Non-specific and dose-related: **headache (commonest), nausea, dizziness, malaise** ("flu without fever"), then confusion, syncope, seizures, **loss of consciousness, coma**, and cardiac ischaemia/arrhythmia. **Clue: multiple household members / co-located people ill together**, symptoms better away from home, winter heater use. "Cherry-red" skin is rare/late. Lactic acidosis and myocardial injury mark severity. **Consider concurrent cyanide** poisoning in enclosed-space fire/smoke inhalation.

## 🩻 Investigations

- **Carboxyhaemoglobin (COHb) by co-oximetry** (venous or arterial) — the diagnostic test; **standard pulse oximetry is falsely normal** (cannot distinguish COHb from O₂Hb).
- ABG/lactate, ECG + troponin (myocardial injury), CBC, electrolytes, glucose, pregnancy test; consider **cyanide** co-exposure (lactate very high in smoke inhalation).
- COHb correlates loosely with severity (higher in smokers at baseline ~ up to 10%); **treat by symptoms, not the number alone.**

## 💊 Management

**1. 100% oxygen immediately — the mainstay.** **High-flow 100% oxygen via non-rebreather mask** (or via ETT if intubated), started **before confirmation**. **Continue until COHb < ~3% AND for at least 6 hours** (longer/extended in pregnancy — fetal CO elimination is slower and fetal Hb binds CO avidly). Remove from the source; support ABCs.

**2. Hyperbaric oxygen (HBO) — for severe/high-risk cases.** Consider/refer for HBO (ideally within ~6–24 h) when there is: **loss of consciousness, neurological signs/deficit, cardiac ischaemia/arrhythmia, metabolic acidosis, COHb > ~25% (some use > 40% as urgent), or pregnancy** (HBO at lower COHb thresholds, e.g. > 15%, or regardless of level per some protocols). HBO aims to reduce **delayed neurocognitive sequelae**; evidence is mixed but favoured for the above high-risk groups. Do not delay 100% O₂ or resuscitation to arrange HBO.

**3. Supportive & specific.** Treat seizures, myocardial ischaemia, and rhabdomyolysis; correct acidosis (usually improves with oxygenation). **Suspected concurrent cyanide** (smoke inhalation + high lactate + soot) → **hydroxocobalamin** (avoid nitrites, which worsen CO by forming methaemoglobin).

**4. Follow-up.** Counsel on and follow for **delayed neurological sequelae** (cognitive/psychiatric/movement disorders within ~days–weeks); ensure source remediation (CO detector, appliance check) before returning home to prevent recurrence/household deaths.

## 🇹🇭 Thailand availability & context

100% oxygen (non-rebreather) and co-oximetry are available in Thai EDs; **hyperbaric chambers are limited to specific centres** (naval/hyperbaric-medicine units), so HBO usually requires transfer — arrange early for high-risk patients while continuing 100% O₂. Thai-relevant sources: **charcoal grills/braziers in enclosed rooms, gas water-heaters in bathrooms, and enclosed-space fires** (consider cyanide co-exposure). Hydroxocobalamin availability varies.

> [!danger] 🚨 Red flags / strict avoidance
> - **Pulse oximetry is falsely normal** — measure **COHb**; suspect CO in headache/altered mentation + a source.
> - **Multiple people ill in the same environment** = CO until excluded.
> - **100% oxygen immediately** (before confirmation), until COHb < 3% and ≥ 6 h; **extended in pregnancy**.
> - **HBO for LOC / neuro or cardiac signs / acidosis / COHb > 25% / pregnancy** — arrange early (transfer), don't delay O₂.
> - **Smoke inhalation + high lactate → concurrent cyanide** (hydroxocobalamin; avoid nitrites).

> [!tip] 🎯 High-Yield Recall
> - CO binds Hb (240×), left-shifts the curve, blocks cytochrome oxidase → tissue hypoxia with **normal SpO₂/PaO₂**.
> - **Diagnose with COHb co-oximetry**; treat by symptoms, not the number alone.
> - **100% O₂ (NRB) until COHb < 3% + ≥ 6 h** (extended in pregnancy); halves CO half-life.
> - **HBO**: LOC, neuro/cardiac signs, acidosis, COHb > 25%, pregnancy.
> - Think **cyanide** in enclosed-space fires (hydroxocobalamin); watch for **delayed neuro sequelae**; fix the source.
