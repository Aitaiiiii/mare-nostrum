# Postpartum Hemorrhage (PPH)

Excessive bleeding after birth (traditionally ≥ 500 mL after vaginal or ≥ 1000 mL after caesarean, or any blood loss causing haemodynamic compromise) — a **leading direct cause of maternal death**, usually preventable and treatable with a rapid, protocolised response. The framework is the **"4 Ts"**, and the commonest cause by far is **uterine atony (Tone)**.

## 🧬 Causes — the 4 Ts

| T | Cause | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Tone** (~70%) | **uterine atony** | overdistension (twins, polyhydramnios, macrosomia), prolonged/augmented labour, chorioamnionitis, high parity, tocolytics |
| **Trauma** | genital-tract lacerations, haematoma, **uterine rupture/inversion** | instrumental delivery, precipitous labour |
| **Tissue** | **retained placenta/products**, placenta accreta spectrum | check placenta completeness |
| **Thrombin** | **coagulopathy** | DIC (abruption, amniotic-fluid embolism, sepsis), pre-existing/anticoagulation, HELLP |

## 🩺 Presentation & assessment

Visible heavy bleeding (revealed) — but **concealed bleeding** (haematoma, intra-abdominal, uterine cavity) can cause shock with little external loss. **Visual estimation underestimates loss** — track vitals (tachycardia and hypotension are late in the young/pregnant), and a rising shock index. Examine for the 4 Ts: palpate uterine tone (boggy = atony), inspect the tract for tears, check placental completeness, and assess for coagulopathy.

## 💊 Management (simultaneous: resuscitate + stop the bleeding + find the cause)

**1. Call for help + resuscitate.** Two large-bore IVs, crystalloid, **cross-match/activate massive-transfusion protocol**, bloods (CBC, coagulation/fibrinogen, group & save), O₂, warm the patient, monitor; catheterise (empty bladder aids tone).

**2. Tone — uterotonics + mechanical (first, as atony is commonest).**
- **Uterine massage / bimanual compression** immediately.
- **Oxytocin — first-line uterotonic** (IV infusion/IM; e.g. bolus then infusion).
- If inadequate: **ergometrine IM** (avoid in hypertension/pre-eclampsia), **carboprost (PGF2α) IM** (avoid in asthma), and/or **misoprostol 800 µg sublingual/rectal**.
- **Tranexamic acid 1 g IV as early as possible (within 3 h of birth)** — the **WOMAN trial** showed reduced death from bleeding; repeat once if bleeding continues.

**3. Trauma / Tissue / Thrombin.** **Repair lacerations**; **remove retained placenta/products** (manual removal/evacuation); **correct coagulopathy** (fibrinogen, FFP, platelets, cryoprecipitate — guided by results/viscoelastic testing); reduce **uterine inversion** immediately; suspect **accreta** if the placenta won't separate.

**4. Escalate if bleeding continues.** **Intrauterine balloon tamponade** (Bakri), examination under anaesthesia, and surgery: **compression sutures (B-Lynch), uterine/internal iliac artery ligation, uterine artery embolisation (interventional radiology), and — last resort — hysterectomy.** Do not delay definitive control in ongoing haemorrhage.

**Prevention:** **active management of the third stage of labour (prophylactic oxytocin)** reduces PPH.

## 🇹🇭 Thailand availability

Oxytocin, ergometrine, carboprost, misoprostol, tranexamic acid, blood products, Bakri balloons, and surgical/embolisation services are available in Thai obstetric units (embolisation at tertiary centres). PPH is a leading cause of Thai maternal mortality, so the drilled response — **massage + oxytocin + TXA + escalate** — and active third-stage management are core skills; misoprostol is a practical, heat-stable option where oxytocin cold-chain is limited.

> [!danger] 🚨 Red flags / strict avoidance
> - **Visual estimation underestimates blood loss**; young mothers compensate then crash — resuscitate early, activate massive transfusion.
> - **Give tranexamic acid early (within 3 h)** — it reduces death from bleeding (WOMAN).
> - **Ergometrine — avoid in hypertension/pre-eclampsia; carboprost — avoid in asthma.**
> - **Concealed bleeding** (haematoma, uterine inversion, rupture) can cause shock with little external loss.
> - Ongoing bleeding → **escalate promptly** (balloon → surgery/embolisation → hysterectomy) — don't delay.

> [!tip] 🎯 High-Yield Recall
> - **4 Ts: Tone (atony, ~70%) · Trauma · Tissue · Thrombin.**
> - **Resuscitate + massage/bimanual + oxytocin (1st-line) → ergometrine/carboprost/misoprostol 800 µg + TXA 1 g IV (< 3 h).**
> - Repair tears, remove retained tissue, correct coagulopathy; reduce inversion.
> - Escalate: **Bakri balloon → B-Lynch/artery ligation/embolisation → hysterectomy.**
> - Prevent with **active third-stage management (prophylactic oxytocin)**.
